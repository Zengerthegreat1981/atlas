# Duplicate pages: English, other modules (proposal only)

Nothing was deleted, merged or edited for this report. Source: the 109 candidate groups in `_gem/lists/dup_candidates.txt`. None of those pairs is already listed in `DUPLICATES_EN_ANTHROPOLOGY.md` (script check).

## Limits

- Not a line-by-line comparison. Decisions rest on title, crumb, type, the first 420 characters of the lede, section headings and a 5-word-sequence overlap score.
- Contradictions were looked for only in A groups, by reading shortened sentences containing dates or numbers (source lists excluded), plus a few targeted searches. B and C pages were not systematically checked. No claim was verified against outside sources.
- "A" mostly means two independently written entries on the same subject in the same role, not copied text. Only Boas, Haraway and Linda Tuhiwai Smith are identical or near-identical.
- Words: as given in the candidate list. Links: number of other `content/en` files (excluding `_merged/`) containing the exact slug, twins included.
- `*` after a slug: a file with that slug also exists under `content/ar`. Check this before choosing which slug to retire; recommendations do not rest mainly on it.
- Before removing a page, move unique material and retarget links, including `related` entries.

## Contradictions and divergences between twins

No clear conflict of dates or major facts was noticed within the limits above. Two points to settle when merging:

1. Znaniecki: `thk-znaniecki` calls the body he presided over in 1954 the "American Sociological Association"; `thk-florian-znaniecki` says "American Sociological Society". The latter is, to my knowledge, the name in use in 1954 (not checked against a source).
2. Boas: `thk-franz-boas` says he positioned himself "against colonialism and racism" and that his work "established cultural relativism as anthropological principle"; `thk-boas` drops "colonialism" and calls the book "a basis for cultural relativism". `thk-boas` reads as the corrected version and also has a section the twin lacks.
