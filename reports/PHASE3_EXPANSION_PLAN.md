## **SECTION 1: PSYCHIATRY EXPANSION (1 → 250 nodes)**

### **Current State**
- **Nodes:** 1 (stub)
- **Target:** 200–250 nodes
- **Rationale:** Clinical field; complements psychology (4,031 nodes); bridges to neuroscience, medical ethics, anthropology

### **Subfields to Open (12 major domains)**

#### **1. Diagnostic Systems & Nosology (20 nodes)**
- History of psychiatric classification
- DSM-5 diagnostic criteria and debates
- ICD-11 international classification
- Dimensional vs. categorical diagnosis
- Neurodiversity framework
- Key thinkers: David Rosenhan, Allen Frances, Nancy Andreasen

#### **2. Neurobiology of Mental Illness (25 nodes)**
- Neurotransmitter systems (dopamine, serotonin, GABA, glutamate)
- Brain circuits and psychiatric disorders
- Genetic and epigenetic contributions
- Neuroimaging findings
- Psychiatric genetics
- Key thinkers: Eric Kandel, Solomon Snyder, Arvid Carlsson

#### **3. Pharmacological Psychiatry (20 nodes)**
- Psychopharmacology principles
- Antipsychotics, antidepressants, mood stabilizers, anxiolytics
- Drug mechanisms and side effects
- Polypharmacy and treatment resistance
- Psychedelic-assisted therapy
- Key thinkers: Paul Greengard, Julius Axelrod, Stephen Stahl

#### **4. Psychotherapy & Psychodynamics (25 nodes)**
- Psychoanalytic theory and practice
- Cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT) foundations
- Humanistic and existential approaches
- Systemic/family therapy
- Third-wave therapies (ACT, mindfulness)
- Key thinkers: Sigmund Freud, Carl Rogers, Aaron Beck, Albert Ellis

#### **5. Trauma & PTSD Studies (18 nodes)**
- Trauma theory and neurobiology
- PTSD diagnostic criteria and manifestations
- Complex trauma (C-PTSD)
- Trauma-informed care
- Historical trauma and intergenerational effects
- Key thinkers: Bessel van der Kolk, Judith Herman, Pierre Janet

#### **6. Mood Disorders (Major Depression & Bipolar) (18 nodes)**
- Major depressive disorder pathology and treatment
- Bipolar spectrum disorders
- Suicidality and risk assessment
- Depression in special populations (perinatal, geriatric, adolescent)
- Key thinkers: Nancy Andreasen, Frederick Goodwin, David Kupfer

#### **7. Anxiety Disorders & OCD Spectrum (15 nodes)**
- Generalized anxiety disorder
- Panic disorder and agoraphobia
- Social anxiety disorder
- Obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD)
- Body dysmorphic disorder
- Key thinkers: Edmund Bourne, David Clark, Paul Salkovskis

#### **8. Psychotic Disorders & Schizophrenia (20 nodes)**
- Schizophrenia subtypes and symptomatology
- Psychotic spectrum disorders
- Prodromal states and early intervention
- Neurochemical theories (dopamine hypothesis and beyond)
- Key thinkers: Kurt Schneider, William Kraepelin, Nancy Andreasen

#### **9. Personality Disorders (15 nodes)**
- Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD) pathology and treatment
- Antisocial Personality Disorder
- Narcissistic and other cluster B disorders
- Trait vs. categorical models
- Key thinkers: Otto Kernberg, Marsha Linehan, Theodore Millon

#### **10. Developmental & Child Psychiatry (18 nodes)**
- Neurodevelopmental disorders (ADHD, autism spectrum)
- Childhood anxiety and depression
- Trauma in childhood
- Developmental trajectories to mental illness
- Key thinkers: Jean Piaget, Mary Ainsworth, Donald Winnicott

#### **11. Cultural Psychiatry & Global Mental Health (20 nodes)**
- Culture-bound syndromes
- Ethnopsychiatry and medical anthropology
- Mental illness in non-Western contexts
- Migration and mental health
- Stigma across cultures
- Key thinkers: Laurence Kirmayer, Arthur Kleinman, Inge-Britt Sretzer

#### **12. Forensic Psychiatry & Law (15 nodes)**
- Criminal responsibility and insanity
- Risk assessment and dangerousness
- Competency evaluations
- Involuntary commitment and civil rights
- Ethics in forensic assessment
- Key thinkers: Jonas Rappeport, James Garbarino, Henry Steadman

### **Distribution (250 nodes)**
- **Schools:** 12 (one per subfield)
- **Thinkers:** 60+ (key figures in psychiatry)
- **Concepts:** 80+ (diagnostic criteria, syndromes, mechanisms)
- **Works:** 50+ (landmark texts, research monographs)

- **Bridge to Psychology:** Mental illness mechanisms, neurobiology, therapy theories

## **SECTION 2: ARAB-THOUGHT EXPANSION (1 → 200 nodes)**

### **Current State**
- **Nodes:** 1 (stub)
- **Target:** 150–200 nodes
- **Rationale:** 
  - Unique Atlas advantage (underrepresented in English-language digital humanities)
  - Rich intellectual tradition spanning 1400+ years
  - Bridges multiple sections (religious-studies, philosophy, historiography, linguistics)

### **Subfields to Open (10 major periods/traditions)**

#### **1. Pre-Islamic Arabian Thought (15 nodes)**
- Jahiliyyah poetry and wisdom traditions
- Pre-Islamic ethics and metaphysics
- Key thinkers: Imru' al-Qais, Al-Shanfara, Zuhayr ibn Abi Sulma
- Transition to Islamic thought

#### **2. Early Islamic & Classical Period (600–1200 CE) (25 nodes)**
- Qur'anic interpretation (tafsir) traditions
- Early jurisprudence (fiqh) schools
- Kalam (Islamic theology) and rationalism
- Arabic grammar and linguistic philosophy
- Key thinkers: Al-Shafi'i, Abu Hanifa, Al-Ghazali, Ibn Sina, Al-Razi
- Works: Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din, Muqaddimah theories

#### **3. Golden Age of Translation & Synthesis (800–1000 CE) (20 nodes)**
- Bayt al-Hikma (House of Wisdom) and translation movement
- Greco-Arabic philosophical synthesis
- Aristotelian logic and metaphysics in Arabic
- Key thinkers: Al-Kindi, Hunayn ibn Ishaq, Thabit ibn Qurra
- Scientific advancement (mathematics, astronomy, medicine)

#### **4. Medieval Islamic Philosophy (1000–1500 CE) (20 nodes)**
- Avicennism (Ibn Sina's synthesis)
- Averroism (Ibn Rushd's defense of philosophy)
- Illuminationism (Suhrawardi's mystical philosophy)
- Mysticism (Sufism) and philosophy integration
- Key thinkers: Ibn Sina, Ibn Rushd, Suhrawardi, Ibn Arabi
- Metaphysics, epistemology, ethics frameworks

#### **5. Islamic Ethics & Political Thought (15 nodes)**
- Qur'anic ethics and virtue traditions
- Islamic jurisprudence (Sharia) as ethical system
- Political authority and governance
- Rights and justice in Islamic thought
- Key thinkers: Al-Mawardi, Ibn Taymiyyah, Al-Ghazali
- Integration with modern human rights discourse

#### **6. Islamic Mysticism & Sufism (18 nodes)**
- Mystical theology (wahdat al-wujud, wahdat al-shuhud)
- Sufi orders and practices
- Love mysticism (Rabi'a al-'Adawiyyah)
- Poetry and spiritual knowledge
- Key thinkers: Al-Ghazali, Rumi, Ibn 'Arabi, Hafiz
- Philosophical foundations of Sufism

#### **7. Linguistic & Literary Thought (15 nodes)**
- Arabic grammar philosophy (nahw)
- Rhetoric and poetics ('ilm al-bayan)
- Semantics and meaning-making
- Literary criticism traditions
- Key thinkers: Sibawayhi, Al-Jahiz, Ibn Qutayba
- Language as reflection of thought

#### **8. Renaissance & Modernization (1850–1950) (20 nodes)**
- Nahda (awakening) intellectual movement
- Confrontation with Western thought
- Educational reform and secularization
- Anti-colonial intellectualism
- Key thinkers: Jamal al-Din al-Afghani, Muhammad 'Abduh, Qasim Amin
- Women's intellectual rights and liberation

#### **9. Contemporary Arab Intellectual Movements (18 nodes)**
- Nasserism and Arab nationalism
- Leftist intellectualism (Marxist traditions)
- Islamic modernism and reformism
- Postcolonial Arab theory
- Key thinkers: Frantz Fanon (adopted), Ali Shariati, Nawal El Saadawi
- Globalization, modernization, identity debates

#### **10. Women's Intellectual Traditions (12 nodes)**
- Women poets and philosophers (pre-Islamic to modern)
- Feminist Arab thought
- Gender in Islamic philosophy
- Women's epistemology and knowledge production
- Key thinkers: Hind bint 'Utba, Rabi'a al-'Adawiyyah, Nawal El Saadawi
- Contributions often erased from traditional histories

### **Distribution (200 nodes)**
- **Schools:** 10 (one per period/tradition)
- **Thinkers:** 60+ (philosophers, theologians, poets, intellectuals)
- **Concepts:** 60+ (philosophical and theological concepts)
- **Works:** 40+ (landmark texts, philosophical treatises)
- **Studies:** 15+ (research areas, contemporary scholarship)
- **Debates:** 5+ (key intellectual controversies)

### **Integration Strategy**
- **Bridge to Religious-Studies:** Islamic theology, Qur'anic interpretation, Sufism
- **Bridge to Philosophy:** Logic, metaphysics, epistemology, ethics
- **Bridge to Historiography:** Periodization, intellectual change, translation movements
- **Bridge to Linguistics:** Arabic grammar philosophy, semantics, rhetoric
- **Bridge to Anthropology:** Cultural context, ritual knowledge, oral traditions
