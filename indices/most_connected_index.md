# Quick Reference Guide - Most Important Terms

Essential psychology terms with highest connectivity and citation frequency.

## Core Psychological Concepts

| Term | ID | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Consciousness | TRM-0001 | The state of being aware of and able to think about one's surroundings, thoughts, and feelings |
| Emotion | TRM-0002 | A natural instinctive state of mind deriving from one's circumstances, mood, or relationships |
| Cognition | TRM-0003 | The mental action or process of acquiring knowledge and understanding through thought, experience, and senses |
| Behavior | TRM-0004 | The way in which one acts or conducts oneself, especially toward others |
| Memory | TRM-0005 | The faculty by which information is encoded, stored, and retrieved |
| Perception | TRM-0006 | The organization, identification, and interpretation of sensory information |
| Learning | TRM-0007 | The process of acquiring knowledge or skill through study, experience, or being taught |
| Motivation | TRM-0008 | The driving force or reason for doing something |
| Stress | TRM-0009 | A state of mental or emotional strain or tension resulting from adverse or very demanding circumstances |
| Anxiety | TRM-0010 | A feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease, often about an imminent event or uncertain outcome |

## Clinical and Therapeutic Concepts

| Term | ID | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Trauma | TRM-0100 | Emotional shock following a stressful event, typically resulting in lasting psychological effects |
| Depression | TRM-0101 | A serious medical condition where a person feels persistently sad and loses interest in activities |
| Attachment | TRM-0102 | The emotional bond between a caregiver and infant that establishes a secure base |
| Psychotherapy | TRM-0103 | The treatment of mental disorder by psychological rather than medical means |
| Resilience | TRM-0104 | The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties; toughness |
| Coping | TRM-0105 | Strategies and behaviors used to manage stress or difficult emotions |
| Mindfulness | TRM-0106 | The quality or state of being aware of something; focused, non-judgmental attention |

## Neuroscientific Terms

| Term | ID | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Neurotransmitter | TRM-0200 | A chemical messenger that transmits signals between nerve cells |
| Synapse | TRM-0201 | A junction between two nerve cells across which impulses pass |
| Amygdala | TRM-0202 | Limbic brain structure crucial for processing emotions, particularly fear |
| Hippocampus | TRM-0203 | A seahorse-shaped brain structure involved in memory formation |
| Prefrontal Cortex | TRM-0204 | Brain region involved in planning, decision-making, and impulse control |
