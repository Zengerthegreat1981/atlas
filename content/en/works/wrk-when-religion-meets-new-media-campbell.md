# When Religion Meets New Media

A book by Heidi A. Campbell, published by Routledge in 2010, an influential work in the field of digital religion studies. It examines how religious communities negotiate the use of the internet and other new media, and proposes a framework for analysing that negotiation.

## Structure and Argument

The book studies how religious communities, including Jewish, Christian and Muslim ones, respond to new media. It argues that the religious response to new technology is neither a deterministic replacement of traditional practice nor simple rejection of it. Rather, it is an ongoing negotiation shaped by each community's history, core beliefs, patterns of authority and textual reference. Campbell frames this approach as the "religious-social shaping of technology," which looks at a community's history and tradition, its core beliefs and patterns, the negotiation process, and the communal framing and discourse surrounding the technology.

## Impact

The book became a frequently cited reference in religion and digital media studies. Campbell later edited *Digital Religion: Understanding Religious Practice in Digital Media* (first edition 2013; second edition 2021 with Ruth Tsuria), which surveys the field more broadly, and developed the idea of "networked religion" in subsequent work.

## Methodology

The framework avoids two pitfalls: excessive technological optimism, the assumption that the internet inevitably "liberates" religion from institutional constraint, and pessimism, the assumption that technology necessarily threatens traditional religion. It proposes instead that the outcome varies with each community's internal structure of authority and its history with earlier technologies such as print, radio and television.

## Heidi Campbell

Heidi A. Campbell is a professor of communication at Texas A&M University and a leading scholar of religion and digital media. She founded the Network for New Media, Religion, and Digital Culture Studies, which brings together researchers from several disciplines.
