# Leopold von Ranke

German historian and professor at the University of Berlin from 1825 until his retirement. He is regarded as the founder of modern academic history as a methodical discipline based on rigorous criticism of primary sources, not as a literary narrative or moral sermon.

## Contributions

- **The motto "as it actually happened" (wie es eigentlich gewesen)**: He formulated it in the preface to *Histories of the Latin and Germanic Peoples* (1824), declaring that the historian's aim is to present the event rather than to judge it or bend it to a moral lesson or a preconceived philosophical scheme.
- **Establishing the university research seminar (Seminar)**: He was the first to organize collective training for graduate students in the direct analysis of a specific primary document, rather than relying on lecture-style narration. The model spread worldwide and remains in use in the training of professional historians to this day.
- **Priority of the document contemporary with the event**: He developed strict criteria for preferring primary sources (diplomatic correspondence, official minutes) over later accounts, and a systematic internal and external criticism to establish the authenticity of each document before relying on it.
- **Rejection of philosophical teleology**: Unlike his contemporary Hegel in Berlin, he insisted that "every epoch is immediate to God," meaning that each era has its own independent value and is not merely a station on an alleged inevitable path of progress.

## Position within the Current

Ranke is the starting point against which academic modern history defines itself, affirmatively or negatively. The French methodical school (Seignobos and Langlois) drew directly on him, while the Annales school (Bloch and Febvre) re-read him critically, charging him with narrowness (confining history to the history of states and diplomacy). Peter Novick later traced how his famous motto, in its American reception, became a positivist doctrine far simpler than his original, more complex position.

## Major Works

- *Histories of the Latin and Germanic Peoples from 1494 to 1514* (1824).
- *History of the Popes* (Die römischen Päpste, 1834–1836).
- *German History in the Age of the Reformation* (Deutsche Geschichte im Zeitalter der Reformation, 1839–1847).

## Selected Quotations

"I only want to show what actually happened" (wie es eigentlich gewesen). (Preface to *Histories of the Latin and Germanic Peoples*, 1824)
