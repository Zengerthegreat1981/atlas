# Rollo May

The bridge. Without him the European tradition remains confined to German and French.

## What He Did

He co-edited the volume that carried European existential analysis into English, and wrote the first serious American treatment of existential anxiety.

## His Key Distinction

Between normal anxiety, inherent in existence, and neurotic anxiety, produced by fleeing from it. A distinction simple in appearance, and the basis of all later practice.

## What Distinguishes Him from Yalom

Broader philosophically and less systematic. For him anxiety is not primarily death anxiety but a confrontation with non-being in general — including the loss of meaning, values, and identity.

## His Other Interests

Love and will, creativity, and power and innocence — topics neglected by those who came after him.

## Major Works

The Meaning of Anxiety (1950) · Man's Search for Himself (1953) · Existence: A New Dimension in Psychiatry and Psychology (co-edited, 1958) · Love and Will (1969) · The Courage to Create (1975)

## Selected Quotations

"The opposite of courage in our society is not cowardice, it is conformity." — The Courage to Create, 1975
