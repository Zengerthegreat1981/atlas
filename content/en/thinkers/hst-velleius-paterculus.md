# Velleius Paterculus

Velleius Paterculus wrote a concise history emphasizing Augustus and the Julio-Claudian dynasty, reflecting a soldier's perspective on Roman history.

## Definition and Historical Significance

Velleius Paterculus (19 BCE-31 CE) was a military officer and historian who wrote a concise history of Rome and other nations. His account emphasized Augustus and the Julio-Claudian dynasty. As a military officer, his perspective emphasized military organization and the army's role in history.

## Historical Context

Velleius lived through the late Republic and early Empire, serving as a military officer under the imperial system. His history reflected military perspective and Augustan ideology.

## Key Contributions and Methodology

Velleius contributed military officer's perspective on history; emphasis on military organization and martial virtue; detailed account of the Julio-Claudian period.

## Theoretical Framework

Velleius organized history chronologically while emphasizing military and political developments.

## Examples and Case Studies

His accounts of military campaigns stress strategic and tactical considerations. His treatment of Augustus emphasizes his military achievements.

## Contemporary Relevance

Velleius provides valuable military perspective on Roman history and important information about the Julio-Claudian dynasty.

## Limitations and Gaps

His concise form limits detail. His military perspective emphasizes martial matters at expense of other dimensions. His accounts reflect military bias.
