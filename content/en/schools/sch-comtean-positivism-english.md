# Comtean Positivism

Auguste Comte's foundational positivist approach establishing sociology as the 'science of society' with systematic laws of social organization and development. Comte argued that just as natural phenomena follow discoverable laws, social phenomena also follow discoverable laws that can be studied scientifically.
