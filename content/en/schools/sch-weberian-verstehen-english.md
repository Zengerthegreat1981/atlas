# Weberian Interpretive Sociology

Max Weber's interpretive sociology emphasizing verstehen (understanding) of subjective meanings and intentional action. Weber developed ideal types as analytical tools and analyzed rationalization, authority types, and religious ethics as sociological phenomena.
