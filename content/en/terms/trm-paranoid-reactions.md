# Paranoid Reactions

Paranoid reactions are a group of pathological responses to emotional stress. They may stand alone, be mixed with other psychopathological states, and even be found in everyday life. The core of the paranoid reaction is the psychological defence of projection. The essence of this defence is the attribution to the external world of those wishes, impulses, feelings and thoughts that are unacceptable
