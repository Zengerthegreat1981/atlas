# Synonyms: logoneurosis; logospasm.

Standard deviation (SD): Statistical measure of variability within a set of values so defined that, for a normal distribution, about 68 per cent of the values fall within one SD of the mean, and about 95 per cent lie within two SD’s of the mean.
