# Munchausen syndrome.

Somnambulism: Sleepwalking. More frequently seen in males than in females, it is most common in children, who generally outgrow it. In adults it is often associated with other psychiatric distur- bances, such as schizoid personality disorder and schizophrenia. In DSM, it is included in sleep- walking disorder. Somnolence: Pathological sleepiness or drowsiness from which the patient can be aroused
