# Rogers, Carl R. (1902-1987)

Founder of client-centred or non-directive psychotherapy, Carl Rogers is considered part of the ‘third force’ in psychotherapy, a force which is characterized in opposition to psychoanalytic approaches on the one hand and behaviourist approaches on the other.
