# Intelligence and Intelligence Testing

The testing of intelligence has a long history (for psychology) going back to the turn of the century when Binet in Paris attempted to select children who might profit from public education. Since that time the notion of intelligence has been the subject of considerable scrutiny, especially by Spearman in Great Britain in the 1930s, and of much and often bitter controversy.
