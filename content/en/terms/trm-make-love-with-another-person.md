# make love with another person.

Mneme Memory: Hence mnemic image, the psycho- logical equivalent of a memory trace. Mnemonic: An aid to memory, which can be achieved in any way, including leaving a note for oneself. There have been several different kinds of mnemonics identified and developed over time. Many of them have to do with forming of mental images which will help the person to remember connections between item, or lists
