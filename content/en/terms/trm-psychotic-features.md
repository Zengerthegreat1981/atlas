# psychotic features

Demand characteristics: The sum total of cues (derived from the manner in which the subject is solicited. The manner in which he is treated by the experimenter, the scuttlebutt about the experiment, the experimental instructions, and, most important, the experimental procedure itself) that communi- cates the purpose of the experiment and the nature of the behaviour expected of the subject.
