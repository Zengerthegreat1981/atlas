# Primary prevention: See preventive psychiatry.

Primary process: In psychoanalysis, the mental activity directly related to the functions of the id and characteristic of unconscious mental pro- cesses. The primary process is marked by privative, prelogical thinking and by the tendency to seek immediate discharge and gratification of instinctual demands. It is seen in infancy and in dreams.
