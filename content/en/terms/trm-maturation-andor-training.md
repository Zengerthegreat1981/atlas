# maturation and/or training.

Test, Arthur Point Scale: A non-verbal performance measure of intellectual ability, consisting of 10 subjects, mainly of the formboard variety. The test is most reliable within the 7 to 13 year age range and is of particular value when the subject’s verbal capacity is compromised by foreign language handicap, speech or hearing defect, or personal and cultural factors.
