# express this distinction.

A child’s intelligence is strongly related to the likelihood that an adequate level of reading will be attained. Therefore there will be children whose reading ability is less than would be expected for their age but is what would be expected for their level of intelligence. In contrast, however, there are also child­ ren who are of normal intelligence but who read less well than would be expected
