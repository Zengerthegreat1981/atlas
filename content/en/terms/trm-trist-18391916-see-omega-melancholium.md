# trist, 1839–1916). See omega melancholium.

Sign, Stiller’s: (Berthold Stiller, Budapest physician, 1837–1922). The presence of floating tenth rib as indicative of a neurasthenic tendency; called also costal stigma. Signal anxiety: Attenuated anxiety that functions as an early warning system for the ego.
