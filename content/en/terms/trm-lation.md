# lation.

Hyperkiness: Excessive, unintentional motor activity of the limbs or any part of the body, appearing spontaneously or in response to stimulation. Hyper- kinesis is a feature of a variety of organic disorders of the central nervous system but may also appear in the absence of a demonstrable localized lesion. 194 Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences Hyperkinetic syndrome of childhood: Disorder
