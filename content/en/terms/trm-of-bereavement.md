# of bereavement.

Suggestion: The process of influencing a patient to accept an idea, belief, or attitude suggested by the therapist. See also hypnosis. Suggestibility: State of uncritical compliance with influence or uncritical acceptance of an idea, belief, or attitude. It is commonly observed among persons with hysterical traits. Suicide: The act of self-inflicted, self-intentioned taking of one’s own life. Alth
