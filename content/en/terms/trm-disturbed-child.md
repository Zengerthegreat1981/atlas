# disturbed child.

Residual: The phase of an illness that occurs after remission of the florid symptoms or the full syndrome. Example: the residual states of infantile autism, attention deficit disorder and schizophrenia. Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences 355 Residual schizophrenia: Schizophrenia in which the patient no longer is psychotic but does have some remaining signs of the illness. Resistance: A con
