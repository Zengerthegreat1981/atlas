# self-limited. See also depression.

Grief reaction: A response by a bereaved person to the loss, that characteristically proceeds from a phase of shock and bewilderment, via a depressive preoccupation with the deceased, to a gradual period of resolution. Deviations from this sequence are common and morbid patterns of grieving may constitute a frank depressive illness. Synonyms: Bereavement reaction; brief depressive reaction Diction
