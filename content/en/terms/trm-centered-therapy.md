# centered therapy.

Unconditioned response: A response which occurs automatically to a particular stimulus, and not have to be learned. For example, pulling the hand away from a burningly hot surface is an unconditioned response: it happens as a reflex, without the need 440 Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences for conscious recognition of what is happening.
