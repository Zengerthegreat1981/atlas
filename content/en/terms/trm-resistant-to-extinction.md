# resistant to extinction.

Avoidant disorder: In DSM-III, a term was used for a disorder of childhood or adolescence characterized by a persistent or excessive shrinking from strangers. In DSM-II, this condition was called withdrawing reaction. Avoidant personality disorder: A personality disorder characterized by low self-esteem, hypersensitivity to rejection, and social withdrawal but a desire for affection and acceptance
