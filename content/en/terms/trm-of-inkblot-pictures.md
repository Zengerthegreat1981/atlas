# of inkblot pictures.

Rosenthal effect: The finding by Robert Rosenthal and others that one’s expectations can have an effect on an outcome that is being observed. Used particularly in connection with the finding that when teachers were told that a group of children were very bright, those children performed better than a similar group that the teachers had been told were all dull.
