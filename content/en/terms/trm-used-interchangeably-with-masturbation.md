# used interchangeably with masturbation.

Oneiromancy: Divination by means of dreams; not to be confused with dream interpretation which makes no claim to be predictive and uses the dream solely as evidence of the dreamer’s state of mind. Oneiro- logy is a rarely used word for the scientific study of dreams. Oneirophrenia: A syndrome described as occurring in acute schizophrenic illness, with some clouding of consciousness and dream-like
