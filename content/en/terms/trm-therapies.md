# therapies.

Self concept: The sum total of the ways in which the individual sees her or himself. Self-concept is often considered to have two major dimensions: a descriptive component, known as the self-image, and an evaluative component, known as self- esteem, although in practice the term is more commonly used to refer to the evaluative side of self-perception.
