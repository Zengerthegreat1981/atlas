# defences.

In 1894, Sigmund Freud suggested that psychopathology was caused by upsetting affects, or emotions, rather than disturbing ideas. Freud observed that the ego’s defence mechanisms can cause affects to become ‘dislocated or transposed’ from particular ideas or people, by processes which Freud later called dissociation, repression, and isolation. However, affects could be ‘reattached’ to other ideas
