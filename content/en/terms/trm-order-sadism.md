# order, Sadism.

Sexual drive: One of the two primal drives or instincts (the other is the aggressive drive) according to Freud’s dual-instinct theory formulation in 1920. Also known as life instinct or Eros, it operates under the pleasure-unpleasure (pleasure-pain) principle, and its main goal is to preserve and maintain life.
