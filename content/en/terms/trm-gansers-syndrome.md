# Ganser’s syndrome.

Privilege: Legal term referring to the legal right of a patient to prevent his physician from testifying about information obtained in the course of treatment. It is the legal formulation of the ethical principle of confidentiality. In some states, infor- mation obtained in the course of the doctor-patient relationship is considered privileged communi- cation. Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sci
