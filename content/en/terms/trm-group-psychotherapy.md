# group psychotherapy.

Group relations theory: See Allport’s group relations theory. Group test: A psychometric test which is administered to several people at once, by a single tester; such as some of the school-type intelligence tests. Group think: The conformity of opinion that arises under certain conditions in decision-making groups; often due to the reluctance of some members of the group to voice criticism. Growt
