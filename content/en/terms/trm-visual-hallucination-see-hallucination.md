# Visual hallucination: See Hallucination.

Visual illusions: Figures which appear to be other than they really are, as a result of the ways in which the brain interprets information. Visual illusions have been extensively studied by psychologists, as it is thought that investigations of the errors of percep- tion can throw light on how normal perceptual processes work.
