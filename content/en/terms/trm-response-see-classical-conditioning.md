# response. See classical conditioning.

Conditioned stimulus: A stimulus which brings about a response as a result of repeated association with an unconditioned stimulus. See also classical conditioning, conditioned response. Conditioning: Procedure resulting in the acquisition or learning of more or less permanent changes in behaviour.
