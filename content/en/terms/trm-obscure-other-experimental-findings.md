# obscure other experimental findings.

Response generalization: The tendency to produce a learned response in conditions which are similar, though not identical to those under which the response was learned. In general, the more similar 356 Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences the conditions are, the stronger the response will be, known as the generalization gradient.
