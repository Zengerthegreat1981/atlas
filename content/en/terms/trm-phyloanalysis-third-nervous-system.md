# Phyloanalysis, Third nervous system.

Bystander apathy: A rather moralistic label applied by social psychologists to the phenomenon that onlookers fail help in emergencies even though they may be upset by what is happening. Concern about bystander was aroused by the case of kitty Genovese who was stabbed to death in New York in 1964.
