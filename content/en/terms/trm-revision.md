# revision.

Statistical inference: The process of using a limited sample of data to infer something about a large population of potentially obtainable data which has not been observed. Statistical significance: Research term referring to an experimental result, based on a sample of obser- vations, that demonstrates an outcome or effect of sufficient magnitude alone in less than 5 per cent.
