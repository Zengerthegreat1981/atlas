# ing, conditioning.

Unconditioned stimulus: A stimulus which automati- cally produces a response in an organism (animal or human being). The term ‘unconditioned’ means ‘not learned’: a stimulus of this kind will operate by reflex, or automatically, with no learning being necessary. It forms the basis for classical condi- tioning as the new, conditioned stimulus becomes linked with the unconditioned one. See condi- ti
