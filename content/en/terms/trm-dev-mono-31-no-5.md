# Dev. Mono., 31, no. 5.

BERNE'S SCRIPT THEORY. The Canadian-born American psychologist/ psychiatrist Eric L. Berne (1910-1970) formulated his script theory concerning personality (ego) development and relationships between individuals (cf: Ad- ler's, 1927, 1937, concept of lifestyle), which states that each person creates a life script early in life as a way of meeting one's needs, and it is usually carried out unknowing
