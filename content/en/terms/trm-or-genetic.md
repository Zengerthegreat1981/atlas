# or genetic.

Innate releasing mechanism (IRM): A term used by Tinbergen to refer to the stimulus which triggered off an instinctive behaviour. Examples are the moving shape which stimulates peeking in a young herring-gull chick and that which provokes ‘freezing’ in turkey chicks.
