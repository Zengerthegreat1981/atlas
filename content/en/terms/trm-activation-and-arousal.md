# Activation and Arousal

The terms activation and arousal have often been used interch­ angeably to describe a continuum ranging from deep sleep or coma to extreme terror or excitement. This continuum has sometimes been thought of as referring to observed behaviour, but many psychologists have argued that arousal should be construed in physiological terms.
