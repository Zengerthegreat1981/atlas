# See ABBA.

Balanced scale: A test or questionnaire in which sources of basis in the items are counterbalanced. For example half of the items should be true and half false, so that any tendency to prefer to answer ‘yes’ does not distort the outcome. Bandura, Albert: Born in 1925 in Alberta, Canada.
