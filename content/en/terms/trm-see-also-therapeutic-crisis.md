# See also Therapeutic crisis.

Therapeutic role: Position in which one aims to treat, bring about an improvement, or provide alleviation of a distressing condition or state. Therapeutic window: The well-defined range of blood levels associated with optimal clinical response to antidepressant drugs, such as nortiptyline. Levels above or below that range are associated with a poor response. Therapist: See Psychotherapist. Thinkin
