# spectrum.

The transduction of radiation into a nervous response takes place in the rods and cones; the former mediate vision in dark­ ness, the latter in light. The latter also are associated with colour vision and high contrast sensitivity. Hence, if they are absent, as happens in certain diseases, vision is severely handi­ capped (Ripps, 1982).
