# ATTRIBUTION THEORY 59

(cf: McArthur, 1972). According to Kelley (1971), a covariation/correlation principle is employed when people infer the causes of events, including the behavior of other people, by observing whether two events vary together or simply occur together (such as lightning and thunder).
