# some controversy.

It is certainly the case that some forgetting is due to diffi­ culties in locating a memory trace rather than its destruction. Most people have the experience of knowing a name which is on ‘the tip of their tongue’ but cannot be recalled. Typically such a name would be recognized immediately from a list of plausible alternatives, indicating that the information had been mislaid rather than destroy
