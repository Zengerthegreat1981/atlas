# Jung maintained that,

The personalities of the doctor and patient are often infinitely more important for the outcome of the treatment than what the doctor says or thinks (although what he says and thinks may be a disturbing or a healing factor not to be underesti­ mated). For two personalities to meet is like mixing two different chemical substances: if there is any combination at all, both are transformed.
