# healthy or wholesome relationships.

Systematic desensitization: A form of behaviour therapy developed by Joseph Wolpe and others in which anxiety-evoking stimuli are presented to the patient while he is in a state of deep muscle relaxation in an attempt to weaken the bond between the stimuli and the anxiety. The technique has proved particularly useful in the treatment of phobias. See also Reciprocal inhibition and desen- sitization
