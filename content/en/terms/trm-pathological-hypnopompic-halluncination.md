# pathological). Hypnopompic halluncination.

Hallucination occurring while awaking for sleep (ordinarily not considered pathological). Kines- thetic hallucination. Hallucination of bodily movement. Lilliputian hallucination. Visual sensation that persons or objects are reduced in size; it is more properly regarded as an illusion (see also Micropsia). Olfactory hallucination. Hallucination involving smell.
