# Psychosomatic Illness

In the broadest sense of the term, all human illness is psycho­ somatic, since the functions of mind and body are closely inter­ woven. Emotional disorders are commonly accompanied by bodily symptoms, and physical illness often leads to patho­ logical emotional responses. Clinical practice reflects this duality.
