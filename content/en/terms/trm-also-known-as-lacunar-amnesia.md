# also known as lacunar amnesia.

Amnesia, neurological: (1) Auditory amnesia: Loss of ability to comprehend sounds or speech. See also Wernicke’s aphasia. (2) Tactile amnesia: Loss of ability to judge the shape of objects by touch. See also Astereognosis. (3) Verbal amnesia: Loss of ability to remember words. (4) Visual amnesia: Loss of ability to recall or recognize familiar objects or printed words.
