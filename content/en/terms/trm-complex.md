# complex.

256 Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences Masculinity-femininity scale: Any scale on a psycho- logical test that assesses the relative masculinity or femininity of the testee. Scales vary and may focus, for example, on basic identification with either sex or preference for a particular sex role. Such scales are strongly influenced by cultural definitions of masculinity and femininity. Masochi
