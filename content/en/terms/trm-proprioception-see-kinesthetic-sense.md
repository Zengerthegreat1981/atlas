# Proprioception: See Kinesthetic sense.

Prosopagnosia: An inability to recognize familiar faces that is not due to impaired visual acquity or level of consciousness. Prototaxic: A term introduced by Harry Stack Sullivan to refer to primitive illogical thought processes. See Primary process. Pseudo aggression: A neurotic defense in which the patient denies his basic masochistic feelings and displays them, instead, as false aggression. Ps
