# Federn, Paul: (1871–1950), Austrian psychoanalyst,

one of Freud’s earliest followers, and the last survivor of the original Wednesday Evening Society. He made important original contributions to psychoanalysis, such as the concepts of flying dreams and ego feelings and he was instrumental in saving the minutes of the Vienna Psychoanalytic Society for subsequent publication.
