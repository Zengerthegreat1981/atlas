# called Rhine cards.

Zero-sum game: In games theory, the class of games in which a fixed quantity of resources is distributed between the players, so that for anyone to do better someone else must do worse. Zero-sum games are of particular interest to social psychologists because it has been found that people operate according to the same principles even when they are not in a zero-sum situation.
