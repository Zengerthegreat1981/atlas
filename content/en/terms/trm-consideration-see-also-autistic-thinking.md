# consideration. See also Autistic thinking.

Descriptive psychiatry: A system of psychiatry focusing primarily on the study of observable symptoms and behavioural phenomena, rather than underlying psychodynamic processes. Kraepelin’s systematic description of mental illness was an early example. See also Dynamic psychiatry. Desensitization: A procedure which will reduce the responsiveness of the subject. Used mostly for behavioural technique
