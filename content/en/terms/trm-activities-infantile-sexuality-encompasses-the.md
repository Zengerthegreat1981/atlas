# activities. Infantile sexuality encompasses the

overlapping phases of psychosexual development during the first 5 years of life and includes the oral phase (birth to 18 months), when erotic activity centers on the mouth; the anal phase (ages 1 to 3), when erotic activity centers on the rectum; and the phallic phase (ages 2 to 6), when erotic activity centers on the genital region.
