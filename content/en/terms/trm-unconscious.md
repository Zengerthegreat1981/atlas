# Unconscious

Perhaps the single most important idea in Freud’s theory is that human beings are influenced by ideas, feelings, tendencies and ways of thinking of which they are not conscious. Freud’s original ‘topography5 of the mind had three divisions: the conscious, the preconscious, and the unconscious.
