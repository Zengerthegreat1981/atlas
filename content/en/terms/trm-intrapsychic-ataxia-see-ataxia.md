# Intrapsychic ataxia: See Ataxia.

Intrapsychic conflict: State of tension arising from the clash of two or more incompatible or opposing forces–for example, wishes, needs, motives, thoughts–operating within oneself. It is also known as intrapersonal conflict. See also Extrapsychic conflict.
