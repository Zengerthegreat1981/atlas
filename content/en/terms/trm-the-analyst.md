# the analyst.

Interpretation of dreams: The title of a book by Freud. Published in 1899 this work a major presentation not only of Freud’s theories about the meaning of dream’s–subject hitherto regarded as outside scientific interest–but also of his concept of a mental apparatus that is topographically divided into unconscious, preconscious, and conscious areas.
