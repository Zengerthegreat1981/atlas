# See mania.

Mania, chronic intellectual: Obs, “ general disturbance of the intellect characterized by the existence of varying unsystematized delusions, accompanied by periods of mental excitement of depression, with more or less incoherence and mental weakness.” (Foster, F.P. Medical Dictionary, 1892–94) Mania, classification of: See classification of mania. Mania, collecting: The morbid impulse to collect.
