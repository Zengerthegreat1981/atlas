# form disorder.

Schizophrenia, latent: A term introduced by Bleuler (1911) to designate a cluster of abnormal person- ality traits which he attributed to an underlying schizophrenic process though positive evidence of schizophrenia was lacking. Closely related concepts are ‘borderline’ schizophrenia and the ‘schizotypal’ personality disorder. In ICD-9, the term is not recommended for general use but a description
