# towards that person.

Recognition: The second form of remembering identi- fied by Ebbinghaus, and one which is used exten- sively by human beings. Ebbinghaus, working with lists of nonsense syllables, demonstrated that material which cannot be recalled may nonetheless be recognized as having been in a previously lear- ned set of information, if it is presented to a subject.
