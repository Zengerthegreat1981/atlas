called for increased multicultural sensitivity in ethical Virtue Ethics (v1); Working Alliance (v2) counseling practices. Becoming competent to work with clients from varied cultural backgrounds is an important goal for ethical counselors, as professionals Further Readings are expected to limit their practice to those areas within which they have competence.
