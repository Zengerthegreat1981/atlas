# Therapeutic alliance: Conscious contractual

relationship between therapist and patient in which each implicitly agrees that they need to work together to help the patient with his problems. It involves a therapeutic splitting of the patient’s ego into observing and experiencing parts. A good therapeutic alliance is especially necessary for the contribution of treatment during phases of strong negative transference. See also Working alliance
