# Sensation and Perception

Sensation and perception refer to the mechanisms by means of which we are aware of and process information about the external world. Aristotle classified the senses into the five categ­ ories of seeing (vision), hearing (audition), smelling (olfaction), tasting (gustation), and the skin senses.
