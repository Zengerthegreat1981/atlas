# of storage system.

Retroactive interference: When new information which is being learned interferes with the ability to recall information which was learned previously. For example, a tennis player who takes up squash may find their tennis deteriorates for a while. See also proactive interference. Retroflexion: A psychoanalytic term used notably by Rado to describe the turning of rage onto oneself. Retrograde amnesi
