# behaviour to evolve.

In Sociobiology, E. O. Wilson extrapolated from Hamilton’s theory to posit that the evolution of social behaviour throughout the animal kingdom including Homo sapiens has followed these rules of kin selection. Of course this position ignores the general criticism that none of the social behaviours discussed have been shown to have a genetic basis and are certainly not under simple genetic control.
