# Zeitgeist best.

Zener cards: A standard set of card used in experimental studies of extrasensory perception. There are usually 25 cards, each bearing one of 5 simple symbols; cross, wave, circle, star or square. 458 Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences In a typical experiment subjects are asked to guess which pattern is on a card that another subject (out of sight) is looking at.
