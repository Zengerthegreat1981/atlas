# disorder, Grief, Major depression.

Depressive position: A Kleinian concept, it describes the position reached (in her scheme of things) by the infants (or by the patient in analysis) when he realizes that both his Love and Hate are directed towards the same object–the mother becomes aware of his ambivalence and concerned to protect her from his hate and to make reparation for what damage he imagines his hate has done.
