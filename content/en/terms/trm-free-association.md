# Free Association

The requirement for free association is often referred to as the fundamental rule of psychoanalysis. The patient in psychoana­ lytic treatment attempts to express in words all thoughts, feel­ ings, wishes, sensations, images, and memories without reser­ vation, as they spontaneously occur. Originally, Sigmund Freud introduced free association to assist in the abreaction of trau­ matic experiences.
