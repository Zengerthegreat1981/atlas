# natives (Clalt).

Comparison level for alternatives (Clalt): In social exchange theory, an individual’s standard used for judging the outcomes that would be received in the next best alternative relationship, or in simply being alone, when outcomes in the present relationship fall below the Clalt, a person will leave the relationship in favour of the alternative.
