# days.

Post-hypnotic suggestion: A suggestion made to someone while they are in a hypnotic state, which concerns behaviour which they will undertake once the hypnotic fugues over. In the case of relatively trivial forms of behaviour, this is often performed by the subject, who typically says that they ‘just felt like doing it’. Post-hypnotic suggestion has sometimes been presented by Hollywood film maker
