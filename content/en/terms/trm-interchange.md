# interchange.

Conversion: A defense mechanism, operating uncon- sciously, by which intrapsychic conflicts that would otherwise give rise to anxiety are, instead, give symbolic external expression. The repressed ideas or impulses and the psychologic defenses against them, are converted into a variety system. These may include such symptoms as paralysis, pain or loss of sensory function.
