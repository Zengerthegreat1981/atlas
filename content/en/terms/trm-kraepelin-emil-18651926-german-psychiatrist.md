# Kraepelin, Emil (1865–1926): German psychiatrist

noted for his pioneering work in psychiatric nosology and classification systems. He differen- tiated between manic depressive psychoses and dementia precox (schizophrenia). One of the last representatives of the predynamic school of psychiatry, he is often considered the father of descriptive psychiatry. See also Descriptive psychiatry.
