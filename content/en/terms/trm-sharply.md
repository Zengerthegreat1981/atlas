# sharply.

Yerkish: An artificial ‘language’ developed during experiments in chimpanzee language training at the Yerkes primate laboratory in Georgia, U.S.A. Initially developed for use with a chimpanzee named Lana, it consists of a series of symbols used in a fairly arbitrary fashion to stand for concepts and conjunctions. There is considerable debate as to just how far Yerkish can be regarded as a language
