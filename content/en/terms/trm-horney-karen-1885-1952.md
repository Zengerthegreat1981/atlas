# Horney, Karen (1885-1952)

Born in Germany in 1885, Karen Horney trained as a Freudian psychoanalyst and was the first important ‘feminist5 critic of Freud’s biological and mechanistic theories. Freud, as has often been noted, created a male-oriented psychology which stressed the psychological consequences of anatomical differences.
