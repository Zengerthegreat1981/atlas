# hyperactivity.

Hyperlexic: Hyperlexic children are those who learn to read extremely quickly, with little apparent difficulty. The opposite of dyslexic. Hypermnesia: Exaggerated degree of retention and recall. It can be elicited by hypnosis and may be seen in certain prodigies; it may be a feature of obsessive-compulsive disorder, some cases of schizophrenia, and manic episodes of bipolar affective disorder.
