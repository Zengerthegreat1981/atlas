# teacher’s transmission of information.

Discrimination: Process of distinguishing between different but often similar stimuli and reacting appropriately. The term also refers more specifically to the occurrence of operant behaviour in environ- ments in which the behaviour has been reinforced but not in those in which it has not been reinforced.
