# behaviour and uses various conditioning

techniques derived form learning theory to modify the patient’s behaviour directly. Behaviour therapy aims exclusively at symptomatic improvement, without addressing psychodynamic cauzation. A major worker in the field was Joseph Wolpe, who developed and popularized the technique of systematic desensitization.
