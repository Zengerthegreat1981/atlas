# Genetic Aspects of Mental Illness

Differences in the prevalence of mental illnesses may be partly explained by genetic factors (breeding effects and higher consanguinity rates), or by differences in ethnic backgrounds. However, environmental factors may also be implicated, while some divergences could be a result of sampling errors.
