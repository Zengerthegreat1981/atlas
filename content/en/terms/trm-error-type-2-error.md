# error. Type 2 error.

Number and letter peg systems: Mnemonic techniques in which to be remembered items are linked with a well-learned set of numbers or letters. Compare method of loci. Nurse, psychiatric: Part of the mental health team, usually in an institutional setting. The nurse works with patients in the hospital milieu; today the psychiatric nurse often carries out individual, family, and group psychotherapy. N
