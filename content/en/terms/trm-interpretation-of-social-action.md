# interpretation of social action.

Ethogenics: A theory and associated methodology for the analysis and explanation of social interaction. Ethologism: The appeal of concepts and findings derived from ethological studies of the behaviour of non-human animals as a source of immediate diagnosis of the dilemmas of the human condition and of prescriptions for change.
