# Culture and Personality, Homewood, 111.

Defences The conceptualization of ego mechanisms of defence presents one of the most valuable contributions that Sigmund Freud and psychoanalysis have made to psychology. Modern psychiatrists, including non-Freudians, define defence mechanisms in a more generic sense, as innate coping styles which allow individuals to minimize sudden, often unexpected, changes in internal and external environment
