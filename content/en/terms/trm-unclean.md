# unclean.

Autonomous dysthymia: A depressive illness in which the mood is qualitatively changed and in which early morning awakening, diurnal mood variation, and overvalued or delusional ideas associated with the parent’s basic worries usually occur. The illness may or may not be some external, but once it begins its course is relatively independent of the causal event and the environment. Autonomous morali
