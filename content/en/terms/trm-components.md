# components.

(1) gf Fluid ability: This is the basic reasoning ability which in Cattell’s view is largely innate (but see below) and depends upon the neurological constitution of the brain. It is largely independent of learning and can be tested best by items which do not need knowledge for their solution. A typical fluid ability item is: is to as is to . . . with a multiple choice of five drawings. O O V An e
