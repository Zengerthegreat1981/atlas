# Abstract thinking: See abstract attitude.

Abstract thought: Thought which uses concepts which do not have an immediate material correspondence such as justice or freedom. In Piaget’s theory of cognitive development, the capacity for abstract thought is only acquired after the age of about 12 years. It is an essential aspect of Piaget’s formal operations stage.
