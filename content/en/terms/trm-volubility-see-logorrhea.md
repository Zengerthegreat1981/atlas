# Volubility: See Logorrhea.

Volly principle: In the auditory system, and in several others sensory systems, the intensity of a stimulus is signaled by means of the rate at which electrical impulses are fired to the brain. This signal can be achieved by each neurone firing very rapidly but owing to the absolute and relative refractory periods, there is a limit to how fast each neuron can fire.
