# worlds.

Repetition–compulsion: Term used by Freud to describe what he believed to be an innate tendency to revert to earlier conditions. The concept was used by him in support of the Death-instinct concept. Since the animate develops out of the inanimate, there is, innate drive, the death-instinct, to return to the inanimate.
