# of significance are occurring.

Apparent motion: A term used to describe visual illusions which provide an appearance of move- ment even when no such movement is actually occurring. Examples of this are found in the phi phenomenon, the water fall effect, and strobos- copic stimuli. Appeasement: A ritualized gesture (including voca- lizations and scents of submission). It is commonly the opposite of threat gestures such as crouch
