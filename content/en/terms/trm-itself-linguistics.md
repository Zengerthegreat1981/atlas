# itself (linguistics).

Pratt, Joseph H.: Boston physician born in 1842 and generally considered to be the first pioneer in group psychotherapy in America. He formed discussion groups amount patients with tuberculosis to deal with the physical aspects of their disease. Later, those groups began discussing the emotional problems that stemmed from the illness. Preconscious: One of the three divisions of the psyche in Freud
