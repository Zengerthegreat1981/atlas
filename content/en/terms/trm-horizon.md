# horizon.

Grief: Alteration in mood and affect consisting of sadness appropriate to a real loss. Normally, it is self-limited. See also depression. Grief reaction: A response by a bereaved person to the loss, that characteristically proceeds from a phase of shock and bewilderment, via a depressive preoccupation with the deceased, to a gradual period of resolution.
