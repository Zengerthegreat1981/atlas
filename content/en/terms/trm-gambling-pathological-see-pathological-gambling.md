# Gambling, pathological: See Pathological gambling.

Game: The psychological uses of this term are similar to the ordinary meaning except that the idea of playfulness is usually absent. So a game is an activity within defined limits in which all of the participants operate according to agreed rules. Much of social interaction can be regarded as a game, with plenty of scope for problems when the rules and the limits of the game are not made explicitl
