# Hyperphrenia: (Hyperfragia) (1) Excessive mental

activity, such as occurs in the manic phase of manic depressive psychosis or in the severe preoccupa- tions association with the neuroses. (2) Intellectual capacity for above the average. Hyperpragia: Excessive thinking and mental activity, generally associated with the manic phase of bipolar affective disorder. Hypersomnia: Excessive time spent asleep. It is not related to narcolepsy.
