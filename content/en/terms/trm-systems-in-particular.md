# systems in particular.

Biological rhythm: Periodic variation in physiological functions. A circadian rhythm shows a periodicity of about 24 hours. An ultradian rhythm has a cycle shorter than 1 day. An infradian rhythm is longer than 1 day. Biopsychology: The study of the biological sources of individual functioning.
