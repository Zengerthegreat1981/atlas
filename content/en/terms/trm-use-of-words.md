# use of words.

Papez circuit: A neural circuit identified in 1957 by the neuroanatomist J.W. Papez. It consists of the mammillary bodies in the hypothalamus, anterior thalamus, cingulated gyrus, entorhinal cortex, hippocampus and their interconnecting fibers. It is believed to be important in mediating emotional experience and behaviour. (Fig. 10) Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences 297 ReticularSystem Se
