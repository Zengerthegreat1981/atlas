# authoritarianism scales.

Gordon Allport’s analysis of prejudice and stereotypy in 1954 began a general movement toward treating stereotypes as a consequence of normal cognitive functioning rather than looking at them as a by-product of frustration or pathological defensiveness. In this, and subsequent treatments, stereotypes have been viewed as the often unfortunate end-products of inevitable and even necessary strategies
