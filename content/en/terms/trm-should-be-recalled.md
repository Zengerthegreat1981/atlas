# should be recalled.

Such a theory is difficult if not impossible to test directly. One would have to show that the occurrence of a thought in an unrecalled dream reduces the likelihood'*of that thought recurring. On the other hand, it is an error to restrict psychology to the study of theories that can be directly verified or falsified; and an error, too, to assume that the issue of the dream’s meaning is closed.
