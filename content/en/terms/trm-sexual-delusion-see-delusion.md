# Sexual delusion: See Delusion.

Sexual deviation: A mental disorder characterized interests and behaviour other than what is cultur- ally accepted. Sexual deviations include sexual interest in objects other than a person, such as bestiality; bizarre sexual practices, such as necro- philia; and other sexual activities that are not accom- panied by copulation.
