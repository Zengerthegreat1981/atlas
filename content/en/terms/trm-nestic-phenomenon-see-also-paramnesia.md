# nestic phenomenon. See also Paramnesia.

James-Lange theory: An early theory of emotion which argued that the experience of emotion arose from the perception of physiological changes in the body, brought about by the emotional stimulus. In other words: the physiological changes occurred first, and the emotion was simply the perception of those changes. See also alarm reaction; Cannon- Bard theory.
