# de Cleramhault’s syndrome (Pure erotomania):

Delusional belief that another person (the object) often of unattainably higher social status loves the patient ( the subject, usually a female) intensely. ‘Primary’ or ‘Pure’ erotomania, is an isolated pheno- menon whereas ‘secondary’ type is much common in the setting of paranoid, manic or other disorder. Decoding: Technical term for the process of under- standing spoken language.
