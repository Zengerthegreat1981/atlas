# approach of a morbid condition.

Programmed learning: A technique for applying operant conditioning to classroom learning. The information is broken down into small units, and presented to the student in such a way that one unit leads naturally on to the next. Each unit involves some kind of simple test question. If the student gets it right, they move onto the next state; if they get it wrong, they go back over the relevant mate
