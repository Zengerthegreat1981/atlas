# See also Folie a deux.

Shell shock: Obsolete World War I term for an acute mental disorder arising as a consequence of combat experience. Shinekeishitsu: A syndrome described by Japanese psychiatrists consisting of obsessions, compulsive perfectionism, social withdrawal, multiple somatic complaints and neurasthenia.
