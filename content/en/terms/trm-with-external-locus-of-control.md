# with external locus of control.

More ambitious multi-trait theories of personality are intended to account for human personality as a whole rather than just one aspect of it. Their aim is to identify the constel­ lation of fundamental traits that constitute the structure of personality, and to explain differences between people according to their location on these dimensions.
