# bodies.

Necromimesis: The delusion in which the patient believes himself to be dead and acts as though he were. Necrophilia: Sexual attraction to or sexual contact with dead bodies. Necrophilism: A morbid desire to be in the presence of dead bodies. Need: Ambiguous term referring to an internal state of unsatisfaction or tension related to a wish, urge, Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences 275 desir
