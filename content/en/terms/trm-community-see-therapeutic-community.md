# Community: See Therapeutic community.

Community mental health: The attempt to bring public health principles to the area of mental health. Community mental health stresses crisis inter- vention in psychiatric emergencies; it attempts to make inexpensive specialized psychotherapy available to poor people; it attempts to resolve community problems that lead to psychological disorder.
