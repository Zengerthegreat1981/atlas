# Group Therapy

Group therapy implies a group of people who, in an optimal environment with a leader or leaders skilled in psychodynamics, interact for the purpose of conflict resolution and social matu­ ration. For such a process to occur, regularly scheduled meet­ ings over an extended period of time are usually regarded as essential.
