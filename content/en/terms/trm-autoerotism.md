# Autoerotism.

Narcissistic personality disorder: Personality disorder characterized by a grandiose sense of self-impor- tance, preoccupation with fantasies of unlimited success, exhibitionistic need for attention and admiration, exaggerated response to criticism or other perceived threats to self-esteem; and distur- bance in interpersonal relationships.
