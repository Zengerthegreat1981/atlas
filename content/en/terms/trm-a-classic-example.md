# a classic example.

Freud’s theory of the unconscious became more complex in the course of his writings. At first he assumed that everything which was unconscious had once been conscious and had been repressed. The paradigmatic example was the subject who under hypnosis could be given some post-hypnotic suggestion, such as to open an umbrella indoors, but told to forget that he had been given that instruction.
