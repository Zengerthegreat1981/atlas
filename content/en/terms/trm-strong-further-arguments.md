# strong further arguments.

Impasse: see therapeutic impasse. Impetus: In psychoanalytic psychiatry, the force or energy behind a particular drive. Implicit personality theory: The ideas about how personality traits are grouped together often taken for granted in everyday living. For example, traits like ambitious may automatically be grouped with aggressive and energetic; or kind could be grouped with gentle and peaceable.
