# also Tangentiality.

Clairvoyance: The perception of objects or events which are beyond the known reach of the senses. It is a particular form of extra-sensory perception distinguished by the fact that it is practiced by medium, a person supposed to have special powers to communicate with and receive messages from distinct or dead people.
