# scious desire seeks gratification.

The classical theory of psychoanalysis presumes that psychic conflict and structure become established during the last stages of infantile development, which is called the Oedipal stage (Freud, 1955, Vol. VII). In relinquishing incestuous desire, the child of approximately age five identifies with the objects and consequently emerges from infancy with a reasonably self- contained psychic structure
