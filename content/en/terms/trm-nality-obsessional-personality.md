# nality, obsessional personality.

Personality disorder, asthenic: Personality disorder characterized by passivity and a weak or inadequate response to the demands of daily life. Lack of vigour may show itself in the intellectual or emotional spheres; there is little capacity for enjoyment. Synonyms: inadequate personality; passive personality.
