# Freud

Freud held several theories about instinct. In an early version he viewed the psyche as subject to biologically based instinctive drives for self-preservation and reproduction; later a single supply of psychic energy was envisaged as giving rise to and becoming dispersed between the psychic structures of the id, ego, and super-ego, with their rival imperatives of appetite, accommodation, and moral
