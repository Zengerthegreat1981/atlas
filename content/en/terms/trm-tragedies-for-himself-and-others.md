# tragedies for himself and others.

The best-known modern criteria for psychopathic personality disorder were developed by Cleckley (1982). Cleckley’s approach stresses the psychopath’s persistent personality features such as untruthfulness and insincerity, poor judgement and failure to learn by experience, pathological egocentricity and incapacity for love, and inadequately motivated antisocial behaviour.
