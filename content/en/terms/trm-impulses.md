# impulses.

Brief psychotherapy: A form of psychotherapy in which the sessions are limited to 10 to 15 in number and during which time attempts to modify behaviour occur. The approach is used in both individual and group settings. Brief reactive psychosis: A DSM category for a psychosis of less than 1 week’s duration with sudden onset after a major stress.
