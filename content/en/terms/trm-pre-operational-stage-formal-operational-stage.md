# pre-operational stage, formal operational stage.

Concrete thinking: Thinking characterized by actual things and events and immediate experience, rather than by abstractions. Concrete thinking is seen in young children; in those who have lost or never developed the ability to generalize as in certain organic mental disorders, and in schizophrenics.
