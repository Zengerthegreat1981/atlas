# Partial insanity: See Monomania.

Participant observation: A research technique in which the research takes a full role in the group being studied, often without the knowledge of the other members. In this way the distortion produced by the presence of an observer is minimized, and the researcher can obtain a fuller appreciation of the experiences of the group. See observational study. 302 Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Science
