# outlined by Eugen Bleuler.

Affect display: A set of physical changes which indicates an emotional state e.g., pilomotor res- ponse in cats, indicating fear and greeting smile in humans, indicating friendliness. Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences 13 Affect-fantasy: Jung’s term for an emotional laden fantasy. Affect-flat: Absence or near absence of any signs of affective expression.
