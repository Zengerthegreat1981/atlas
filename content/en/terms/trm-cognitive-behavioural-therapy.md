# Cognitive-Behavioural Therapy

Cognitive-behavioural interventions are an attempt to preserve the demonstrated efficiencies of behavioural therapy within a less doctrinaire context and to incorporate the cognitive activi­ ties of the client in the efforts to produce therapeutic change (Kendall and Hollon, 1979).
