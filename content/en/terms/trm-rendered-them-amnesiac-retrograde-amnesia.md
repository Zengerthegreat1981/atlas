# rendered them amnesiac. Retrograde amnesia

usually occurs after some form of brain damage, but can happen in a minor way after concussion. It is not uncommon for people who have been in an accident involving severe concussion to lose all memory of the few minutes leading up to the accident. See also amnesia. Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences 357
