# child and parent domains.

The reported internal consistency reliabilities for the Brian D. Johnson and Yan Li PSI appear to be adequate. The coefficient alpha for the child domain was .90, with subscales ranging from See also Attachment Theory (v4); Child Maltreatment (v1); .70 to .83. The coefficient alpha for the parent domain Conduct Disorder (v1); Ethics in Computer Aided was .93, with subscales ranging from .70 to 84.
