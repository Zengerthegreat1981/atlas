# Unexplained, London.

Pavlov, Ivan Petrovich (1849-1936) The great Russian physiologist and founder of the study of conditioned reflexes, I. P. Pavlov, was the great-grandson of a freed serf and son and grandson of village priests. His sixty- two years of continuous and active research on what he came to call ‘Higher Nervous Activity’ profoundly and immutably altered the course of scientific study and conceptualization
