# physicist. Psychologist, philosopher.

Fechner’s Law: A principle in psychophysics, which states that the sensation experienced by an individual increases as a logarithmic function of the stimulus intensity. In other words, that the physical increase in stimulation required for a perceived increase in intensity is not constant, but systematically greater for higher intensities.
