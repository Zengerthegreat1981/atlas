# with neurological damage (Luria, 1979).

In 1931 and 1932 Luria led two expeditions to Uzbekistan and Kirghizia to examine the intellectual abilities of peasant communities under the impact of collectivization. Their findings were generally consistent with Levy-Bruhl’s notion that socio­ cultural differences in cognitive behaviour reflected different stages of intellectual development.
