# PSYCHOPATHOLOGY, THEORIES OF.

ALL-OR-NONE LAW/PRINCIPLE. This principle, discovered by the American physiologist Henry Pickering Bowditch (1840-1917) while he was studying cardiac muscle (Warren, 1934), states that in any single nerve or muscle fiber the response to a stimulus above threshold level is maximal, independent of the intensity of the stimulus, and dependent only on the condition of the cell at the moment of stimula
