# ality.

Failure to thrive, FTT: A condition of poor growth in infants, usually defined as being below the third centile ( i.e., in the bottom 3% for the stage, sex, and population). In some cases there is a psycho- logical problem which accounts for the poor growth but in the majority of cases there is no organic cause and the condition is called “non-organic failure to thrive”.
