# phase, Phallic phase, Regression.

Fixed action pattern: Complex sequences of behaviour that are genetically pre-programmed so that all members of the species show the behaviour when it is needed. Neither learning nor practices are needed for the behaviour to be performed perfectly. Fixed action patterns have been intensively studied by ethologists, and involve sequences of beha- viour which have been inherited as a complete unit.
