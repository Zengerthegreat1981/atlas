# trichotillomania, pyromania.

In general, mania stresses behaviour and action, whereas the suffix ‘philia’ emphasizes the feeling, attitude, disposition, or preference. Another suffix, used much less frequently, is ‘lagnia’, which emphasizes the erotic element in the craving or activity; most words with this suffix refer to what DSM term paraphilias.
