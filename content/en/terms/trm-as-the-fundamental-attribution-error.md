# as the fundamental attribution error.

Attributional style: The theory that individuals tend to believe in particular kinds of causes for a wide range of effects. Styles may vary in the extent to which they incline towards stable causes (ones which are unlikely to change in the future), global causes (affecting lots of things) and internal external causes (such as character situation).
