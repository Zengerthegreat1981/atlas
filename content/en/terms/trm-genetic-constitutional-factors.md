# genetic-constitutional factors.

Syndrome, intermetamorphosis: Delusional conviction that various people in one’s environment have been transformed physically and psychologically into other people. Intermetamorphosis differs from Fregoli’s phenomenon, which also involves false identification, in that it includes false physical resemblance in addition to false recognition.
