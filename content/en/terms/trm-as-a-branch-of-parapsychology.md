# as a branch of parapsychology.

Clang association: Association or speech directed by the sound of a word, rather than its meaning. Punning and rhyming may dominate the person’s verbal behaviour. It is seen most frequently in schizophrenia or mania. Also known as clanging. Classical concept: A term referring to the classification of human concepts following work by J.S. Bruner and others on the development of thinking. Classical
