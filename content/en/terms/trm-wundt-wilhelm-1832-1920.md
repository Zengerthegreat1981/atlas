# Wundt, Wilhelm (1832-1920)

Wundt, a medically-trained German academic, was a professor of philosophy at the University of Leipzig and is remembered chiefly as a pioneer of experimental psychology. His establish­ ment of modest facilities for experimental psychological research by some of his students in 1879 is conventionally regarded as marking the foundation of the world’s first psycho­ logical laboratory.
