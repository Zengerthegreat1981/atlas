# smaller dose.

Drug withdrawal syndrome: State associated with the discontinuation of the taking of a drug, ranging from severe, as specified for alcohol (delirium tremens), to less severe states characterized by one or more symptoms such as convulsions, tremor, anxiety, restlessness, gastrointestinal and muscular complaints, and mild disorientation and memory disturbance. Synonym: abstinence
