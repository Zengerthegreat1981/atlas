# isolated.

Isolation: In psychoanalysis, a defense mechanism involving the separation of an idea or memory from its attached feeling tone. Unacceptable ideational content is thereby rendered free of its disturbing on unpleasant emotional charge. See also Alien- ation, Relatedness. Isonome: A signal or pathway in the brain that has similar effects on several different agencies. Isophilic: Term used by Sulliva
