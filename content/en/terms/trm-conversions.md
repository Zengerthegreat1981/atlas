# conversions.

Totem: Anthropological term for animal, plant of other object which is venerated by a particular tribe or community and which it treats as a symbol of itself or as its protector. In Freud’s Totem and Taboo (1911), the speculative theory, the totem symbolizes the primal. Father who was murdered when his sons rebelled against his mastery of the primal Horde. Totem and Taboo: Title of a book by Freud
