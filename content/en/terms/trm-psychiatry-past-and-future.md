# Psychiatry - Past and Future

Psychiatric illness is not new to modern society. In the Hippo­ cratic writings (400 B.C.) there are clear descriptions of the major psychiatric disorders. Throughout the centuries psycho­ pathology was described, explained, and classified by the great physicians of the time. The degree of sophistication, or lack thereof, paralleled that for medicine in general. There was no autonomous discipline
