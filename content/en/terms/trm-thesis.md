# thesis.

Hypothetico-deductive method: The technique of investigation outlined by Popper as being central to the scientific method. It consists of investigating by means of the formulation of an explicit hypo- thesis containing an explicit prediction as to what would happen in a given situation. An empirical investigation would then be set up to test the hypothesis, i.e., to see if the prediction were true
