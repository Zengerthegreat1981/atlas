# gation neurosis: traumatic neurosis; post-traumatic

neurosis. Competency to stand trial: Ability to be tried in a court of law. A person is competent to stand trial when at the time of the trial he (1) understands the nature of the charge and the potential consequences of conviction and (2) is able to assist his attorney in his defense.
