# individuals.

Disposition: A tendency to behave in a particular way, when used by developmental or clinical psycho- logists the term implies an inherited tendency, and is used interchangeably with predisposition. When used in the context of motivation and personality it is a general term for any relatively stable behavi- oural tendency and no genetic basis is implied.
