# in different ways.

Synesthesia: Condition in which the stimulation of one sensory modality is perceived as sensation in a different modality, as when a sound produces a sensation of colour. Syntaxic mode: The mode of perception that forms whole, logical, coherent pictures of reality that can be validated by others.
