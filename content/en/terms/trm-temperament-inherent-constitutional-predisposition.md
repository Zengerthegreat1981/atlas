# Temperament: Inherent, constitutional predisposition

to react in a certain way to stimuli. Variations is temperament are evident very early in life. Tender mindedness: A personality characteristic put forward by William James, and later elaborated by H.J. Eysenck: characterized by a gently, optimistic and idealistic approach to the world. Its opposite, tough mindedness, is characterized by a harsher, more pessimistic approach. Tension: Physiological
