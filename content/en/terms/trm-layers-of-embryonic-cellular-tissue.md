# layers of embryonic cellular tissue.

Construct: A term used in personal construct theory to define concepts in a precise way. It is proposed that our cognitive system is made up of bipolar constructs as illness-health and honest-dishonest. A large part of the theory is concerned with the relationships between constructs, e.g., a particular individual may have the idea that honest people tend also to be healthy. Constructive memory: T
