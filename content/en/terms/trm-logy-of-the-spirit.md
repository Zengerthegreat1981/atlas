# logy of the spirit”.

312 Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences Phenomenon, Napalkov: (A.V. Napalkov, Russian neurophysiologist). In exception to the usual; conditioned reflex experiments occurring in some phobic patients in which the conditioning stimulus (e.g., a traumatic event) does not immediately produce a fear reaction; instead the fear increases in time rather than being extinguished as it ordinarily would
