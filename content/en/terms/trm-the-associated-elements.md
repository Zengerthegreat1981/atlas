# the associated elements.

There was much disagreement over the ‘laws’ or conditions of the association of ‘ideas’. It was universally accepted that frequency and contiguity (two or more ‘ideas’ often occurring together or in close succession) constituted a basic condition, for example ‘table’ and ‘chair’ are said to be associated because these two ‘ideas’ are frequently experienced in conjunction.
