Conditional positive regard: A concept introduced by Carl Rogers, which refers to the satisfaction of the basic need for positive regard in human beings. The term ‘conditional positive regard’ refers to approval, love or respect given only as a result of the individual behaving in appropriate’, or socially acceptable ways.
