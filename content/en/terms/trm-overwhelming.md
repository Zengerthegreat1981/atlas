# overwhelming.

Catastrophic stress: A reaction to exceptionally severe physical or mental stress, characterized by a breakdown of coping behaviour, intense anxiety and shock. The term has also been applied to the state of agitation and helplessness exhibited by patients with cerebral damage when confronted with tasks beyond their competence (Goldstein, 1878–1965) See also acute reactions to stress. 62 Dictionary
