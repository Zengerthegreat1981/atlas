# Cognitive Science

According to Drever’s (1964) Dictionary of Psychology, ‘cognition’ is ‘a general term covering all the various modes of knowing - perceiving, imagining, conceiving, judging, reasoning’. It picks out those forms of abstract thinking and problem solving which are based upon the manipulation of either linguistic symbols (propositions) or iconic symbols (images).
