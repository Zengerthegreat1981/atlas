# Klein, Melanie (1882-1960)

Born to an intellectual Jewish Viennese family, Melanie Klein had intended to study medicine until an early engagement and marriage intervened. Then, in her thirties, when she was a housewife and mother of three, she discovered Freud’s writing. Entering analysis with Ferenczi, she began an analytic career.
