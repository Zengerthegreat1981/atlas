# by Bem (1972).

Self-persuasion: The modifications of a person’s beliefs to become consistent with what they observe about their own behaviour. Self-realization: Psychodrama technique in which the protagonist enacts, with the aid of a few auxiliary egos, the plan of his life, no matter how remote it may be from his present situation.
