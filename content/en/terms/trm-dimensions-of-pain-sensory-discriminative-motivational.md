# dimensions of pain: sensory-discriminative, motivational-

affective, and cognitive-evaluative. Recent physiological evidence suggests that each is subserved by a physiologically specialized system in the brain. Recognition of the multidimen­ sional nature of pain experience has led to the development of a paper-and-pencil questionnaire (the McGill Pain Question­ naire) to obtain numerical measures of the intensity and qual­ ities of pain (Melzack, 1975).
