# actually to determine them.

Locus of control: A concept at the core of a social learning theory developed by Rotter in the 1950s. It refers to the belief that a person has about where social reinforcements originate: whether they are internal to the person, or external. Someone with an internal locus of control will tend to believe that marks on an easy depend on the amount of effort and ability applied to writing it. Someon
