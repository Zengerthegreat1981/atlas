# linguistic Abilities.

Test, right and wrong: See responsibility, criminal. Test, Rorschach: (Hermann Rorschach, Swiss psychia- trist, 1884–1922) A projective test consisting of ten inkblots of varying designs and colors, which are shown to the subject one at a time with the request of interpret them. Its purpose is to furnish a descrip- tion of the dynamic forces to personality through an analysis of the formal aspects
