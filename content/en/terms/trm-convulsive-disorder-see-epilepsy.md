# Convulsive disorder: See Epilepsy.

Convulsive therapy: A form of therapy involving a group of techniques that induces seizures. In the strictest sense the seizures are patterned electrical discharges that are termed central seizures and that can be measured by use of an electro-encephal- ograph.
