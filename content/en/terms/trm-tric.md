# tric.

Psychiatrist: A medical doctor whose speciality is the study and treatment of mental disorders. Psychiatry: The branch of medicine that deals with diagnosis, treatment and prevention of mental dis- orders. Psychic determinism: Freudian concept that all mental phenomena have specific antecedent causes, often operating on an unconscious level. Psychic energizer: Any Antidepressant drug. Psychoactive
