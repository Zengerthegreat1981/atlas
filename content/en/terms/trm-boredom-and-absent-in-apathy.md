# boredom and absent in Apathy.

Bottomup processing: Perceptual processing which is initiated by the characteristics of the stimulus and leads on to higher forms of cognitive activity, as opposed to top down processing which begins from the higher levels. Bouquet de maladies: The distinctive odour said to be characteristic of psychiatric patient. Bovarism: Failure to differentiate phantasy and reality.
