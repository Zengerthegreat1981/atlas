# See also chromosome, gene.

Heritability estimate: A figure which purports to state the proportion of influence exerted by genes on the individual’s development. Despite the fact that many developmental geneticists and psychologists (e.g., Hebb) have demonstrated unequivocally how inseparable genetics and the environment are, such figures continue to be constructed.
