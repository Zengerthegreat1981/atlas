# SENSATION AND PERCEPTION 391

Sensory systems can act independently or in conjunction with other senses. The ‘taste’ of food results from the combination of inputs from olfaction, gustation, the skin senses, and kinesthesis. This can be demonstrated by comparing the taste of foods when the nasal passages are blocked.
