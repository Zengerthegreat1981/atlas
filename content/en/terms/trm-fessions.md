Parapsychology: Branch of psychology that deals with extranormal events and behavioural phenomena that are not accounted for or explained by the tenets and laws of present-day conventional science. Examples include clairvoyance, precognition, telepathy, and psychokinesis. See also extra- sensory perception. Metapsychology, Psycho- kinesis.
