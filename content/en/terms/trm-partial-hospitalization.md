# Partial hospitalization.

Weltanschauung: (German) World outlook; conception of reality; philosophy of life. Weyer, Johann (1515–1588): Dutch physician consi- dered by some to be the first psychiatrist. His interest in human behaviour led to his writing De Praestigiis Daemonum (1563), a landmark in the history of psychiatry. White, William Alanson (1870–1937): American psychiatrist famous for his early support of Psychoana
