# CONFLICT, THEORIES OF 115

CONFLICT, THEORIES OF. The term conflict is an extremely broad concept used to refer to any situation where there are mutually antagonistic events, motives, behaviors, impulses, or purposes (Reber, 1995). In the area of learning and motivation psychology, Miller (1944), Miller and Murray (1952), and Miller (1959) developed a precise formulation of conflict theory based on some prelim- inary ideas
