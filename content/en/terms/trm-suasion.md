# suasion.

Oral: Appertaining to the mouth (a) Oral character– Character displayed by persons with fixations at the oral stage. Typical oral character traits are optimism and pessimism, generosity, moodiness, depression, elation, talkativeness, greed and the tendency to engage in wishful thinking (b) Oral erotism–Sensuous please derived from the mouth (c) Oral sadism–Pleasure in hurting by biting.
