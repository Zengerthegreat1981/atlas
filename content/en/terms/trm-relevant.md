# relevant.

Set-weight: A pre-determined body weight, which seems to form the ‘natural’ weight of the animal concerned. The idea of set-weight arose from studies of the hypothalamus, in which it was observed that rats with lesions in particular areas of the hypothalamus would eat more than usual.
