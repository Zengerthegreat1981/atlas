# Luria, Alexander Romanovich (1902-1977)

Alexander Romanovich Luria, the Russian pioneer of neuro­ psychology, was born in Kazan in Soviet Central Asia, and died in Moscow. After graduating in social sciences from the University of Kazan in 1921, he entered the Kazan medical school.
