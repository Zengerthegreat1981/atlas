# reproduction.

Heterolalia: The substitution of meaningless or inappropriate words for those meant or untended, malapropism. Heteronomous morality: The second of Piaget’s stage of moral development, this is also known as the ‘moral realism’ stage. At this point, morality is considered to be subject to the laws of others; in other words, the child accepts as right and proper the rules given by authority. Heterose
