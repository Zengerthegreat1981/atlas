# Thought deprivation: See Blocking.

Thought disorder: Any disturbance of thinking that affects language, communication, or thought content. Thought disorder is the hallmark feature of schizophrenia. Its manifestations range from simple blocking and mild circumstantiality to profound loosening of associations, incoherence, and delusions. See also Communication disorder, Language disorder.
