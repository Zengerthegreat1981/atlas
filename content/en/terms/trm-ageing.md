# Ageing

The study of age-related changes in cognitive processes has received fresh impetus as the proportion of elderly people in the population of Western societies continues to increase. It is important that these changes should be recognized, understood and taken into account so as to enable the elderly to cope with a modern environment and continue living a full life of work
