# Personality

Personality (from the Latin persona, an actor’s mask) is an ill- defined concept embracing the entire constellation of psycho­ logical characteristics that differentiate people from one another. There is no consensus on its precise definition: in 1937 Gordon W. Allport quoted more than fifty distinct definitions, and the list has grown considerably since then.
