# The Effectiveness of SST

Does it work? For socially inadequate neurotics, SST does a little better than behaviour therapy aimed to reduce anxiety: SST improves the skills, and may later reduce the anxiety. The worse the patients are, the more sessions are needed. Mental patients of all kinds can be helped by SST, preferably as part of a larger package.
