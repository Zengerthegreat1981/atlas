# criticized, notable by Labour.

Coding: Also referred to as encoding, the term is generally taken to refer to ways in which infor- mation is represented cognitively e.g., for storing in memory of for association with other infor- mation. Memories may be coded in a variety of ways, using many different modalities (e.g., kinaesthetic, or enactive coding, visual or iconic coding , auditory coding). See also representation, schema.
