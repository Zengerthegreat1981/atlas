# several generations.

Cultural diffusion: The spread of cultural traits through contact across societies. Culture fair tests: Tests that reduce cultural bias by incorporating knowledge and skills common to many different cultures and socio-economic groups. Culture free and culture fair tests: During the 1960s and early 1970s considerable efforts were made to develop psychometric tests. e.g., I.Q. and person- ality test
