# Reich, Wilhelm (1897–1957): Austrian psychoanalyst

who emigrated to the United States in 1939. He is best noted for his theory of neurosis, which he believed was associated with incomplete release of tension and energy during orgasm. He coined the term “orgone” for a kind of universally perva- sive physical and biological energy that was typified by the pent-up libidinal energy that sought free expression and release through orgasm.
