# anger is defensive.

Deficiency motive: A motivation that arises because of a perceived deficiency of some kind. The deficiency can range from physiological (e.g., food) to higher needs, such as that for recognition. Deficiency motives are distinguished from ‘abundancy motives’ in which it is judged that the organism is trying to acquire more of the material than is needed for comfortable survival. 106 Dictionary of P
