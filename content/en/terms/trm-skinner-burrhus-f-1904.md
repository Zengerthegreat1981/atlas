# Skinner, Burrhus F. (1904- )

A behaviourist and the most prominent figure in contemporary American psychology, Skinner has devoted most of his professional career to studying the effects of the consequences of behaviour on behaviour. He received his Ph.D. from Harvard in 1931, and from 1948 to 1975 was a professor there. His approach is descriptive and inductive; he is unconcerned with the physiological, mental, or affective
