# circumstances.

Adjustment disorder: Mild and transient disorders lasting longer that acute reactions to stress, which occur in individuals of any age without apparent pre-existing mental disorder. Such disorders are often relatively circumscribed or situation specific are generally reversible and usually last only a few months. They are usually closely related in time and content to stresses such as bereavement.
