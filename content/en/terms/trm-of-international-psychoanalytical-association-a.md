# of International Psychoanalytical Association. A

profile author, he is most noted for his three volume biography of Freud. See also Rationalization. Judgement: Mental act of comparing or evaluating choices within the framework of a given set of values for the purpose of electing a course of action. If the course of action chosen is consonant with reality or with mature adult standards of behaviour, judgement is said to be intact or normal; judge
