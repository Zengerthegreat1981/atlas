# Erythrophobia: Fear of blushing.

ESB: The usual abbreviation for a form of direct electrical stimulation of the brain which appears to function as a powerful reinforcer of behaviour and to give highly pleasurable sensations. Experiments conducted in the 1960s seemed to imply that there was a direct ‘pleasure centre’ in a particular region of the hypothalamus.
