# disorder.

For any particular professional social skill, such as selling, teaching, or supervising working groups, some people are far less effective than others. Some salespersons may sell 25 per cent of what the better ones sell, poor supervisors may generate four times as much absenteeism and labour turnover, and much less output than better supervisors.
