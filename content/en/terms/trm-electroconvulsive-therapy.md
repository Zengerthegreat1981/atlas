# Electroconvulsive Therapy

Electroconvulsive therapy (ECT) is essentially the induction of a cerebral seizure by application of an electrical stimulus to the scalp. The convulsive procedure was introduced after clinical observations suggested sudden improvement in psychiatric pati­ ents after spontaneous convulsions.
