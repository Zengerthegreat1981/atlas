# from the higher levels.

Bouquet de maladies: The distinctive odour said to be characteristic of psychiatric patient. Bovarism: Failure to differentiate phantasy and reality. Bovina fames: (L. “oxlikehunger”) Bulimia. Bradykinesia: Slowness of motor activity with a decrease in normal spontaneous movement. Bradylalia: Abnormally slow speech, common in depression. Bradylexia: Inability to read at normal speed. Brain: A gene
