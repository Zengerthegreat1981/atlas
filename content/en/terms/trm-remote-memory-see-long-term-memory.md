# Remote memory: See Long-term memory.

Remotivation: A group treatment technique used with withdrawn patients in mental hospitals. REM sleep: Stage of sleep during which dreaming occurs and the sleep exhibits coordinated rapid eye movement (REM). The electroencephalogram demonstrates a desynchronized pattern of cerebral activity. It accounts for one-fourth to one-fifth of total sleep time.
