# enced.

Play therapy: A type of therapy used with children in which the child reveals his problems on a fantasy level with dolls, clay, and other toys. The therapist intervenes opportunely with helpful explanations about the patient’s response and behaviour in language geared to the child’s comprehension. See also Activity group therapy.
