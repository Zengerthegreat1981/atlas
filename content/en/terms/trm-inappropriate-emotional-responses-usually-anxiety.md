# inappropriate emotional responses, usually anxiety.

The basic procedure is to present weak forms of the feared stimulus while using stronger forms of a stimulus is then gradually increased in strength without triggering the fear response. The standard procedure is called ‘systematic desensitization’ and is an example of counter conditioning.
