# Naughten rules.

Competition: Struggle for the possession or use of limited goods, concrete or abstract. Gratification for one person largely precludes gratification for another. Complex: A group of interrelated ideas, mainly uncon- scious, that have common emotional tone. A complex strongly influences the person’s attitudes and behaviour.
