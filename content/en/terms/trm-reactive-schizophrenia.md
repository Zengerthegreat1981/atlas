# Reactive schizophrenia.

Prodrome: An early symptom of a disease. It often serves as a warning or premonitory sign of the approach of a morbid condition. Programmed learning: A technique for applying operant conditioning to classroom learning. The information is broken down into small units, and presented to the student in such a way that one unit leads naturally on to the next. Each unit involves some kind of simple test
