# terms of attribution theory.

Learning: A relatively permanent change in knowledge behaviour or understanding that results from experience. Innate behaviours, maturation and fatigue are excluded. Learning has been claimed as the core phenomenon of psychology though in practice, the field often seems to have operated by producing a theory and then defining learning as being whatever that theory explains.
