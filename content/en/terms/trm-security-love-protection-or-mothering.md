# security, love, protection, or mothering.

Dependency needs: Vital needs for mothering, love, affection, shelter, protection, security, food, and warmth. May be a manifestation of regression when they reappear openly in adults. Dependent personality disorder: A personality disorder characterized by lack of self confidence, a tendency to have others assume responsibility for significant areas of one’s life, and a subordination of one’s own
