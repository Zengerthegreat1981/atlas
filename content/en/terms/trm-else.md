# else.

Autogenic: Originating from the self; self-initiated e.g., autogenic training in which the individual is training to have internal control of their own relaxation. Autohypnosis: Hypnosis which has been self-induced. Many forms of hypnotherapy concentrate on the development of the individual‘s own skills in autohypnosis, so that they can develop strategies for coping with stressful events. Autokine
