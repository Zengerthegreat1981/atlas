# levels for alternatives (Clalt).

Social facilitation: The finding that performance is usually improved by the presence of others. Simple and well rehearsed tasks are most likely to be facilitated, so if the presence of others is a source of arousal, the phenomenon follows the Yerkes- Dodson law. Social inhibition: In social groups, the retardation action caused by the presence of other people. Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sc
