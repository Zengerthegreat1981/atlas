# the skin.

Receptor site: A location on the dendrite of a neurone, opposite a synaptic knob, which is sensitive to and readily absorbs a specific chemical. The appro- priate chemical is released into the synaptic cleft from vesicles on the synaptic knob of the opposing neurone, and functions as neurotransmitter, render- ing the receiving neurone more or less ready to fire.
