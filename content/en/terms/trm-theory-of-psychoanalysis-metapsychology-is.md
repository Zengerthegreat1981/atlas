# theory of psychoanalysis. Metapsychology is

branch of theoretical or speculative psychology that deals with theories, hypotheses, or phenomena that are largely beyond the realm of empirical verification. Examples of such topics include mysticism; the origin, purpose, and structure of the mind; the philosophical nature of the mindbody interrelationship; the place of mind in the universe. See also parapsychology. Meyer, Adolf (1866–1950): Ame
