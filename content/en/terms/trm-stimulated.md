# stimulated.

Sense of self: A person’s feeling of individuality, uniqueness, and self direction. Sensitive period: A time period during development in which a given capacity or form of learning can be acquired more easily. Sensitive periods are distinguished from critical periods by the fact that the capacity can be acquired outside the set period, though with greater effort. Sensitivity training group: Group
