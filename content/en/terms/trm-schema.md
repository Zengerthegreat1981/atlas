# schema.

Coefficient of correlation: A statistical term referring to the relation between two sets of paired measurements. Correlation coefficients—which may be positive, negative, or curvilinear, depending on whether the variations are in the same direction, the opposite direction, or both directions, can be computed in a variety of ways.
