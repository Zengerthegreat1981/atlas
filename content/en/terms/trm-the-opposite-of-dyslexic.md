# The opposite of dyslexic.

Hypermnesia: Exaggerated degree of retention and recall. It can be elicited by hypnosis and may be seen in certain prodigies; it may be a feature of obsessive-compulsive disorder, some cases of schizophrenia, and manic episodes of bipolar affective disorder. Hyperorexia: Extreme appetite. See also Bulimia. Hyperphrenia: (Hyperfragia) (1) Excessive mental activity, such as occurs in the manic phase
