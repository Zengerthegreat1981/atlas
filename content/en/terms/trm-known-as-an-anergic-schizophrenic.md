# known as an anergic schizophrenic.

Burnout: A stress reaction developing in person working in an area of unrelenting occupational demands. Symptoms include impaired work performance, fatigue, insomnia, depression, increased suscepti- bility to physical illness and reliance on alcohol or other drugs of abuse for temporary relief.
