# phase dysphoric disorder.

Syndrome, prisoner of war (POW): Psychopathologic manifestations occurring in prisoners of war, presumably a reaction to capture and imprisonment. Various types of reaction have been described, among them a syndrome of withdrawal, apathy, and sometimes death, which has been likened to the anaclitic depression reported by Spitz in hospi- talized or otherwise deprived children.
