# spoken. The development of voice-recognition

systems forms a major area of research in the field of artificial intelligence, but represents no easy task, owing to wide differences in articulation shown by different people. Some success has been achieved in the development of systems which can ‘learn’ patterns of speech used by a particular person. This is usually achieved by the individual concerned reading out a set of key words and phrases
