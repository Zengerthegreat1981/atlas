# Consciousness

The behaviourists took the view that science must ignore reports of conscious experience as unreliable, and deal only with publicly observable phenomena. ‘Consciousness’ was, accord­ ingly, no more amenable to scientific investigation than concepts like ‘ghost’ and ‘soul’.
