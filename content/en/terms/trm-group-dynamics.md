# Group Dynamics

‘Group Dynamics’ was originally the term used, from the 1930s to the early 1950s, the characterize the study of changes in group life by Kurt Lewin and his followers (Lewin, 1943). Gradually, however, the term lost its restricted reference and came to be more or less synonymous with ‘the study of small groups’. It is in this broader, second, sense that it will be used here.
