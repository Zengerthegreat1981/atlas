# Polymorphous perverse sexuality: Psychoanalytic

conceptualization of sexuality in the human infant, for whom sexual gratification can be achieved by homosexual, heterosexual, or inanimate stimulation of several body zones. All humans, therefore, have the potential to develop sexual perversions in adulthood, depending on how the sexual drives are channeled developmentally. Polyphagia: Pathologic overeating. Also known as bulimia. Polysomnography
