# confusion, reactive, consciousness, clouded.

Synonym: confusional state. Confusional state, acute: Short lived transient psy- chotic condition, lasting hours or days. Unless specified as ‘reactive’ confusion, the term refers to organic states e.g., delirium or twilight state. Synonyms: acute psycho organic syndrome; acute organic reaction.
