# involved in muscle action.

Achievement: The successful reaching of goal. Used particularly to refer to real life successes and when evaluating a person’s life. Achievement motivation: The motivation to accomplish valued goals and to avoid failure. This concept became important as motivation theory became less dominated by physiological drives.
