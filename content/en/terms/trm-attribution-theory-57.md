# ATTRIBUTION THEORY 57

ATTRIBUTION THEORY. The Austrian-American psychologist Fritz Heider (1896-1988) was preeminent in the formulation of balance theory in the study of attitudes (i.e., people are motivated to maintain balance, harmony, or "cognitive consonance" among their attitudes, perceptions, and beliefs; Heider, 1958; Newcomb, 1981; cf: state of imbalance, disharmony, or "cognitive dissonance"; Festinger, 1957)
