OF. AUBERT PHENOMENON. See VISION/SIGHT, THEORIES OF. AUDITION/HEARING, THEORIES OF. In general, the audition theories attempt to explain how physical sound vibrations are transformed into the neural impulses that are the basis of hearing (Bekesy & Rosenblith, 1948; Bekesy, 1957).
