# contribute to these complex sensations.

Spatial orientation depends on the integration of visual, vestibular and proprioceptive information, all of which contribute to the maintenance of erect posture and location. The interactive nature of the sensory systems subserving spatial orientation is responsible for the fact that overactivation of the vestibular sense can lead to the illusory sensation that the visual world is moving.
