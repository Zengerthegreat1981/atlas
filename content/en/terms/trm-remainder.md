# remainder.

(5) Intensification of interpersonal relatedness, with an increase in receptivity and suspension of critical judgement. Individuals capable of experiencing some or all of these changes associated with a shift into the hypnotic-trance state may learn to employ them as tools in facilitating therapeutic change. This applies especially to people with disorders which involve the psychosomatic interface
