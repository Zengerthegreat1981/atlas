# Sensory and Motor Development

In describing the stages of intellectual growth from birth to maturity, Piaget (1953) emphasized the initial and continuing importance of motor activity. In his view, the basis for later cognitive growth is constructed in the first two years by the production of physical rather than mental transformation of reality.
