# Hull, Clark L. (1884-1952)

An American neobehavioural psychologist whose influence within psychology was most profound during the 1940s and 1950s, Clark L. Hull was himself influenced by Darwin’s theory of evolution, Pavlov’s concept of delayed or trace-conditioned reflexes, and Thorndike’s law of effect.
