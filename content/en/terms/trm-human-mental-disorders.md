# human mental disorders.

Psychology: Traditionally defined as ‘the science of mind’ but of recent years, and increasingly, the science of behaviour’. Qualifies of ‘psychology’ refer either to specialized branches of the subject- abnormal, normal, animal, human, child, genetic (developmental, industrial, social, clinical, academic, educational – or to different systems of thought – behaviouristic Gestalt, psychoanalytical)
