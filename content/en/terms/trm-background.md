# background.

Three-cornered therapy: See Co-therapy. Three Essays on the Theory of Sexuality: Title of a book by Freud published in 1905. It applied the libido theory to the successive phases of psycho- sexual maturation in the infant, child, and adolescent. It made possible the integration of a vast diversity of clinical observations and pro- moted the direct observation of child development. Tic: Involuntary
