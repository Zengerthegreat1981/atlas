# adults.

Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children (WISC): Intelligence test assessing intellectual functioning in children aged 5 to 15. Wechsler Preschool and Primary Scale of Intelligence (WPPSI): Intelligence test assessing intellectual functioning in children aged 4 to 6½. Wednesday Evening Society: A small group of Freud’s followers who in 1902 started meeting with him informally on Wednesday evenings
