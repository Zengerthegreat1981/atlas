# disorders in children.

Psychosocial development: Developmental progress of a person with regard to social relations and social reality as primarily described by Eric Erikson. Specified developmental tasks characterize successive chronological periods from infancy through maturity. The major tasks and their periods are: trust versus mistrust (infancy), autonomy versus doubt (toddler), initiative versus guilt (preschool),
