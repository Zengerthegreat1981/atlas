# the object.

One of Allport’s views which was less well received was that individuals are characterized by their own unique traits. A more commonly accepted notion is to treat traits as dimensions, which are applicable to most, if not all people, and which are able to capture the uniqueness of each individual according to their particular combinations and relative strengths.
