# material.

Intermission: In psychiatry, the interval between attacks of a particular syndrome. When it is not certain that the symptoms will return, the interval is called a remission. See also Remission. Intermittent reinforcement: Any schedule of reinforce- ment in which the response is not always rein- forced. Internationalization: Making something part of oneself.
