# Szondi’s theory of genotropism (q.v.).

Test, thematic apperception (TAT): A projective technique, originally described by Morgan and Murray in 1935, which focuses primarily on the dynamics of interpersonal relationships. In its present form (the third set to be used since 1935), it consists of a series of 31 pictures that depict a Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences 427 number of social situations and interpersonal relations.
