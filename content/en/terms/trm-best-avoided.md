# best avoided.

Trend of thought: Thinking that centers on a particular idea associated with an affective tone. Triad: Interactional relationship among three persons. Its prototype, the father-mother-child relationship, may evolve projectively in group therapy. Triad, anal: The group of three prime, or outstanding traits of the so-called anal character: (1) Obstinacy (2) Parsimony (3) Pedantic orderliness. Tricho
