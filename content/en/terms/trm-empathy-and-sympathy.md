# Empathy and Sympathy

Empathy has been conceived as being a cognitive process or an emotional-cognitive one. The former conception relates primarily to an individual’s intellectual understanding of another’s ideation and feelings, and has led to studies of the accuracy of predicting another’s responses to questionnaire items and of other adjustments about others.
