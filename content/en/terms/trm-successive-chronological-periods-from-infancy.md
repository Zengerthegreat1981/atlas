# successive chronological periods from infancy

through maturity. The major tasks and their periods are: trust versus mistrust (infancy), autonomy versus doubt (toddler), initiative versus guilt (preschool), industry versus inferiority (school age), identity versus identity diffusion (adole- scence), intimacy versus isolation (young adult- hood), generativity versus self absorption (adult- hood), integrity versus despair (mature age).
