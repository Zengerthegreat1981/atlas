# group session.

Accessible and inaccessible: A patient is said to be accessible if the psychiatrist or analyst succeeds in making rapport with him, and inaccessible if he fails. The term is pseudo-objective, since it implies that the capacity for rapport is a constant, identical in every examining physician. In general memotics are deemed to be accessible and psychotics inaccessible. Accident proneness: Susceptib
