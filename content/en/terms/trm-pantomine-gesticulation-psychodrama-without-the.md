use of words. Papez circuit: A neural circuit identified in 1957 by the neuroanatomist J.W. Papez. It consists of the mammillary bodies in the hypothalamus, anterior thalamus, cingulated gyrus, entorhinal cortex, hippocampus and their interconnecting fibers.
