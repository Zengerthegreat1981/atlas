# absence.

During the Great Depression of the 1930s the psychological impact of unemployment was documented in over 100 studies (summarized in Eisenberg and Lazarsfeld, 1938). They showed the suffering of the unemployed through abject poverty and lack of work; they were often without public support and depended on charity.
