# pool their suggestions.

Brainwashing: The technique of operating total control over a person’s environment with a consistent application of deprivation, debilitation and dread (the three Ds), so that the victim become amenable to adopting a completely new belief system or ideology. The process may depend on some form of identification. It usually refers to systematic efforts to indoctrinate non-believers. Brain waves: Ov
