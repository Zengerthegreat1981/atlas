# Traditionally, hallucinogens have formed an

integral part of religious and social ceremonies in many parts of the world. In the west they are normally used as recreational drugs, although there have been several instances of artists and creative writers utilizing their effects to obtain special insights for their work, and one or two investigations of their usefulness in certain kind of therapy.
