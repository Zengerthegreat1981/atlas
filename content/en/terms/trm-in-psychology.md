# in Psychology

A ABC THEORY. The American psychologist Albert Ellis (1913— ) developed rational-emotive therapy (RET), which is a directive, confrontational psycho- therapy designed to challenge and modify clients' irrational beliefs thought to cause their personal distress (Ellis, 1961, 1977, 1987).
