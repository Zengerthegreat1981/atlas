# ality disorder.

Intuitive substage: In Piaget’s theory of cognitive development. The second of two substages of the preoperational stage, from roughly 4 to 7 years of age; it is characterized by unsystematic reasoning based on perceptual appearances. Compare preconceptual substage. Inversion: Term for homosexuality used by Freud, who distinguished three types; absolute, amphigenous, and occasional. See also Homos
