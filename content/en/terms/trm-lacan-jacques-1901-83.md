# Lacan, Jacques (1901-83)

Jacques Lacan has been called the ‘French Freud’. He was probably the most original and certainly the most controversial European psychoanalyst of the post-Second World War era. Lacan was a scathing critic of the ‘American’ developments in psychoanalysis which moved away from Freud’s unconscious to what was called ‘ego psychology’.
