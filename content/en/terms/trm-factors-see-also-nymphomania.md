# factors. See also Nymphomania.

Scapegoat theory: The idea that prejudice arises from people seeking to blame others for their own negative circumstances. According to scapegoat theory, poor living conditions, economic depre- ssion and frustrating situations lead people to react in hostile ways to others; and this reaction is likely to focus on any individuals who are present but don’t belong to the person’s own peer group.
