# perception; thinking.

Motivation Motivation, as the word implies, is what moves people. If most of psychology deals with ‘How’ questions, like ‘How do people perceive?’ or ‘How do people learn habits?’ the field of motiv­ ation is concerned with more fundamental ‘Why?’ questions.
