# psychiatry. See also community psychiatry.

Community psychiatry: Psychiatry focusing on the detection, prevention and early treatment of mental disorders and social deviance as they develop in the community rather than as they are perceived Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences 77 and encountered at large centralized psychiatric facilities. Particular emphasis is placed on the environmental factors that contribute to mental illness. C
