# Fixed-interval reinforcement: A reinforcement sche-

dule in which reinforcements, or rewards, are given only after a set period of time since the last reinforcement became available. After a suitable acquisition period, this method of administering reinforcement tends to produce a high level of responding around the time of the reinforcement, and a low rate of responding at other times. It has low resistance to extinction.
