# See also Drive, Instinct.

Impulse control: Ability to resist an impulse, drive, or temptation to perform some action. Impulse control disorder: A mental disorder in which there is weak impulse control. The impulsive behaviour is usually irresistible, pleasurable, and aimed at obtaining immediate gratification, without regard for the consequences of the behaviour.
