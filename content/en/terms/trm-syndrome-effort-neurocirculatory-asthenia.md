# Syndrome, effort: Neurocirculatory asthenia.

Syndrome, Ekbom’s: Restless legs syndrome (q.v.); when it occurs spontaneously it is often associated with low serum iron. Akathesia as a side effect of neuroleptic administration is similar in appearance and may also be associated with iron deficiency.
