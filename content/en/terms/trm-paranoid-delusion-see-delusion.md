# Paranoid delusion: See delusion.

Paranoid disorder: A mental disorder characterized by persecutory or grandiose delusions and related disturbances in mood, thought, and behaviour. In DSM-III the paranoid disorders include paranoia, shared paranoid disorder, acute paranoid disorder, and a typical paranoid disorder.
