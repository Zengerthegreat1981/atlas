# messages.

Informed consent: Permission to perform a medical or research procedure which includes; (1) a rational understanding of the nature of the proceedings; (2) the foreseeable risks; (3) the expected benefits (4) the consequences of withholding consent; (5) available alternative procedures; and (6) that consent is voluntary.
