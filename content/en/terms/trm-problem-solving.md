# Problem Solving

Problem solving is a major function of thought and has long been researched in cognitive psychology. Since the information­ processing approach to cognitive psychology became theoret­ ically dominant in the early 1960s, thinking has generally been regarded as a serial symbol manipulation process that makes use of a very limited working memory, supported by an exten­ sive long-term memory.
