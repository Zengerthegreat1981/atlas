# substitute.

Love need: A term used by some humanistic psycho- logists to refer to the need for affection or positive regard from others, which is seen as a fundamental part of human nature. LSD (lysergic acid diethylamide): A potent psycho- togenic drug discovered in 1942. LSD produces psychotic-like symptoms and behaviour changes, including hallucinations, delusions, and time space distortions. Luria, Alexan
