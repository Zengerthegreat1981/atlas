# it.

Most tests have until now been paper-and-pencil, with only a small proportion involving other types of material or apparatus. Future developments are likely to include the production of computerized versions of existing tests and of new tests designed to benefit from computer technology, for example, tests involving the display of dynamic material on the visual display unit. Alan Jones Department
