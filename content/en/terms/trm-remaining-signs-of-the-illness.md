# remaining signs of the illness.

Resistance: A conscious or unconscious opposition to the uncovering of unconscious material. Resis- tance is linked to underlying psychological defense mechanisms against impulses from the id that are threatening to the ego. Resistance to extinction: How long a learned response will carry on without any further reinforcement. Resistance to extinction is often used as a measure of operant strength,
