# following such condition(s).

22 Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences Amnesia, localized: Partial loss of memory; amnesia restricted to specific or isolated experience. It is also known as lacunar amnesia. Amnesia, neurological: (1) Auditory amnesia: Loss of ability to comprehend sounds or speech. See also Wernicke’s aphasia. (2) Tactile amnesia: Loss of ability to judge the shape of objects by touch.
