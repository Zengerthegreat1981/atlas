# Word approximation.

Paraphilia: Type of psychosexual disorder. The term refers to sexual deviation characterized by persis- tent and recurrent sexual fantasies, often of an unusual nature, without which imagery erotic arousal or orgasm is not attained. The fantasies generally involve themes of suffering, humiliation, sexual activity with nonconsenting partners, or preference for a nonhuman object for sexual arousal.
