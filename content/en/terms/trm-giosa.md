# giosa

Drugs: Cocainomia (cocaine), etheromania (ether, inhalants), opiomania (opiates), toxicomania Eating, food: Allotriophagy (unnatural food, such as thread), bulimia, opsomania (sweets), poly- phagia Enemas: Klismaphilia Family, upbringing: Ecomania, oikiomania Filth, excreta: Coprolagnia, coprophilia, myso- philia, urolagnia, urophilia Fire, firesetting: Pyrolagnia, pyromania
