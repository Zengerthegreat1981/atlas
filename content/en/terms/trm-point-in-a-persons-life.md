# point in a person’s life.

Crisis intervention: A brief therapeutic approach used in emergency rooms of general or psychiatric hospitals that is ameliorative, rather than curative, of acute psychiatric emergencies. Often treatment factors focus on environmental modification although interpersonal and intrapsychic factors are also considered. Individual, group, family, or drug therapy is used within a time limited structure
