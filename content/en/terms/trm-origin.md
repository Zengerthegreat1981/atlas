# origin.

Speech act: A term introduced by Buhler (1934) and subsequently taken up by Austin (1962) who stresses that in saying things speakers are actually doing things. Speech disturbance: A term that encompasses any of a variety of language and communication disorders not due to impaired function of speech muscles or organs of articulation.
