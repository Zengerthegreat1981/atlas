# storage.

Encopresis: A disorder, most common in children in which the main manifestation is the persistent voluntary or involuntary passage of formed faces of normal or near-normal consistency, into places not intended for that purpose, in the individual’s own sociocultural setting. Sometimes the child has failed to gain bowel control, and sometimes the child has gained control but becomes encopretic again
