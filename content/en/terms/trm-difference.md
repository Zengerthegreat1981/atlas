# difference.

Relaxation training: A range of techniques to bring about a relaxed state in the subject. Usually used as a component in therapy, for example in maintaining a relaxed state in a phobic patient as they approaches the feared object. Many of the techniques used in psychotherapy are based on methods developed for meditation, such as yoga, or are variations on hypnotic induction procedures. 352 Diction
