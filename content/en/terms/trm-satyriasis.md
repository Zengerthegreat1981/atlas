# satyriasis.

Dictionary of Psychology & Allied Sciences 283 O Obesity: Excessive weight. Obesity is usually defined in terms of body weight being a certain percentage above the ideal weight for that person’s age, sex and height. The percentage varies but is often either 15% or 30%. This vagueness is not crucial as there is no absolute standard for ‘ideal weight’ which is largely a cultural judgement. Object: T
