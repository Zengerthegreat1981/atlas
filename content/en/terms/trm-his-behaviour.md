# his behaviour.

Latency phase: Period of psychosexual development after the phallic phase and succeeded by the genetal phase, extending from about age 5 to the beginning of adolescence. During the latency phase there is an apparent ceszation of sexual preoccupation and a blockade of libidinal inpluses, and boys and girls are inclined to choose friends and join groups of their own sex.
