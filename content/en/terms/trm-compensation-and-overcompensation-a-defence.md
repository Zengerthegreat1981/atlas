# Compensation and overcompensation: A defence

mechanism in which an individual substitutes one activity for another in an attempt to satisfy frustrated (see frustration) motives. It usually implies failure or loss of self-esteem in one activity and the compensation for this loss by efforts in some other realm of endeavour.
