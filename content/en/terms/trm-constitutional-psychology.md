# Constitutional Psychology

Constitutional psychology is, at the same time, an obvious and widely accepted feature of psychological thinking, and a controversial view held by relatively few psychologists. It may be defined as the study of the relation between, on the one hand, the morphological structure and the physiological functioning of the body and, on the other hand, psychological and social functioning.
