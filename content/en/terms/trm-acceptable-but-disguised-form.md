# acceptable but disguised form.

Distractible speech: Pattern of speech in which the person repeatedly changes subjects in response to nearby stimuli. Distractibility: Inability to maintain attention; shifting from one area or topic to another with minimal provocation. Distractibility may be a manifestation of organic impairment or it may be a part of a function disorder such as anxiety states, mania or schizophrenia. Distributed
