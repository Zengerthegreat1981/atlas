# individual behaviour. Environmental psychology

includes the study of such factors as territoriality and personal space, ergonomic design, and the physical attributes of surroundings. Environmentalism: The doctrine that all significant determinants of behaviour are to be found in the environment. Strict behaviourism is one version of environmentalism.
