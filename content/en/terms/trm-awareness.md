# awareness.

Many of the people who came to Peris for therapy were not sensitive to bodily sensations and compensated through overintellectualizing. To Peris, bodily and emotional expressions were positive, natural processes, not to be feared or kept under control, and the techniques he used were directed at eliciting these processes.
