# manifestation of separation anxiety.

Schreber case: One of Freud’s cases. Daniel Paul Schreber’s published autobiographical account, entitled Memoirs of a Neurotic (1903), was analyzed by Freud in 1911. The analysis of those memoirs permitted Freud to decipher the fundamental meaning of paranoid processes and ideas, especially the relationship between repressed homosexuality and projective defenses.
