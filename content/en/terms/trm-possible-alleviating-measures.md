# possible alleviating measures.

Significance level: The arbitrarily selected probability level for rejecting the null hypothesis, commonly 05 or .01. Significant differences: When statistical tests show that a given difference is not likely to have occurred by chance. In many behavioural studies, the likelihood of an event occurring less frequently than 1 in 20 times (p<.05) is considered the minimal acceptable significance leve
