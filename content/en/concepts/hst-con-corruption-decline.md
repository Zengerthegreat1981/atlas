# Corruption and Institutional Decline

Ancient historians analyzed how corruption and moral degradation led to institutional decline and fall.

## Definition and Core Concept

Corruption as a historical cause figured prominently in Roman historiography. Sallust analyzed political corruption in the Republic. Livy explained the Republic's decline partly through moral corruption. The Roman Empire's later decline was attributed to military corruption and moral decay. Corruption explained why once-powerful institutions failed.
