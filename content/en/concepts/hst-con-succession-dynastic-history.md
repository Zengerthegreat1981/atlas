# Succession and Dynastic Continuity

Ancient historians understood that succession struggles determined political stability and historical continuity.

## Definition and Core Concept

Succession and dynastic continuity were central concerns in ancient historiography. Who would succeed a ruler shaped historical outcomes. Imperial historians like Tacitus and Dio Cassius devoted considerable attention to succession struggles. The stability or instability of succession affected the entire realm.
