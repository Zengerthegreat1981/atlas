# Women and Gender in Ancient Historical Writing

Ancient historians largely excluded women from political and military history, creating significant gaps.

## Definition and Core Concept

Ancient historiography, focused on public political and military affairs, largely excluded women. Women rarely appeared except as queens, mothers of important men, or in stories illustrating moral points. This exclusion created major gaps in historical understanding. Modern historians have worked to recover women's history from fragmentary evidence.
