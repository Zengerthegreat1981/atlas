# Comparative Historiography in Antiquity

Comparative approaches in ancient historiography involved examining parallel developments in different regions to understand universal patterns.

## Definition and Core Concept

Comparative historiography in antiquity involved examining parallel developments in different regions or time periods to understand universal patterns and causal processes. Plutarch's paired biographies exemplified comparative method. Polybius compared different states' constitutions and military organizations. Comparative analysis allowed historians to move beyond chronicle of unique events to understanding patterns.
