# Slavery and Unfree Labor in Ancient Societies

Ancient historians rarely focused on slavery despite its fundamental importance to ancient economies.

## Definition and Core Concept

Slavery was fundamental to ancient Mediterranean civilization, but ancient historians paid it limited attention. They noted slave rebellions but did not systematically analyze slavery's role in economic and social organization. This gap in ancient historiography reflects the perspectives and concerns of historians who themselves benefited from or accepted slavery.
