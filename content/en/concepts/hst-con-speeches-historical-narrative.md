# Speeches in Historical Narrative

Speeches in historical narrative served multiple purposes—revealing character, conveying political arguments, and demonstrating rhetorical education.

## Definition and Core Concept

Speeches in historical narrative represented ancient historians' attempt to capture political discourse and reveal character and motivation. Historians like Thucydides composed speeches for historical figures, acknowledging that they were rhetorical compositions while claiming to represent the sense of arguments. Speeches revealed political positions, strategic thinking, and moral philosophy. They illustrated how key figures thought about crucial decisions.
