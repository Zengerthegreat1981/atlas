# Libertas and Freedom in Roman Historiography

Roman historians valued libertas (freedom) as a central political ideal and analyzed its presence and loss.

## Definition and Core Concept

Libertas in Roman thought referred to freedom from tyranny and participation in decision-making. Historians like Livy and Tacitus analyzed the Republic's freedom and its loss under empire. The tension between libertas and imperium (command/empire) was central to understanding Roman political history.
