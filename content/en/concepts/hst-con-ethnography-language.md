# Ethnography of Language and Communication

Ancient historians noted language differences and sometimes discussed how languages affected communication and understanding.

## Definition and Core Concept

Language was important in ancient historiography's cross-cultural encounters. Historians noted language barriers between peoples. In some cases, historians tried to understand how language shaped thinking. However, systematic linguistic analysis was not developed in antiquity.
