# Regression Narratives and Historical Decline

Some ancient historians narrated history as progressive decline from earlier virtue and stability.

## Definition and Core Concept

Regression narratives presented history as decline from a past golden age. Roman historians sometimes presented the transition from Republic to Empire as decline from republican virtue. The past was idealized; the present was viewed as corrupted. This pattern reflects philosophical assumptions about progress and decay rather than objective historical analysis.
