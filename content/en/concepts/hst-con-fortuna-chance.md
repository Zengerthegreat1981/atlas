# Fortune and Chance in Historical Causation

Ancient historians acknowledged that chance and unpredictable fortune affected historical outcomes alongside human agency.

## Definition and Core Concept

Fortune (tyche in Greek, fortuna in Latin) represented the unpredictable element in history—chance events that no one had planned or caused. Ancient historians recognized that fortune could reverse even the most carefully laid plans. Polybius analyzed how fortune affected military outcomes. The existence of chance and unpredictability qualified deterministic theories of causation.
