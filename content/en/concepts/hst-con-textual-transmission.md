# Textual Transmission and Manuscript Corruption

Ancient texts survived through manuscript copies, each subject to errors and alterations that complicate historical interpretation.

## Definition and Core Concept

The transmission of ancient texts from original composition to modern editions involved multiple manuscript copies, each potentially introducing errors, omissions, or alterations. Scribes made mistakes in copying; later editors added commentary or corrected perceived errors; textual traditions diverged. Understanding how texts were transmitted is essential for interpreting ancient historiography.
