# Spiritual Crisis

A spiritual crisis is the collapse of meaning-making structures—religious faith, ideological commitments, or existential narratives that once organized and justified life. This differs from clinical depression in that it is a philosophical confrontation with meaninglessness.

## The Function of Crisis

A spiritual crisis is not purely pathological. It may represent a necessary dissolution of immature or inauthentic meaning-systems in service of developing more robust ones. Someone whose faith collapses may be in genuine existential pain, yet this dissolution may open the possibility of more authentic engagement with meaning.

## Therapeutic Stance

The existential therapist does not aim to restore the abandoned meaning-system but to help the person navigate the void and discover what meaning can be created or found. This may involve grief for the lost certainty and gradual reconstruction of values and coherence.
