# Genealogy and Dynastic Continuity

Ancient historians used genealogy and dynastic succession to organize and understand political history.

## Definition and Core Concept

Genealogy (family lines and descent) was important in ancient historiography for understanding succession, legitimacy, and political continuity. Dynastic organization provided frameworks for understanding how power passed between rulers. Genealogical accuracy was important for establishing legitimacy and understanding inheritance of power.
