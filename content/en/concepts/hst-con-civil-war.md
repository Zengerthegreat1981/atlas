# Civil War and Internal Conflict

Ancient historians studied how internal conflicts and civil wars disrupted political order.

## Definition and Core Concept

Civil wars in Roman history received careful attention from historians. The conflicts between Caesar and Pompey, the civil wars after Augustus's death, and internal instability in the later empire all received detailed treatment. Understanding what caused civil wars and how they affected political development was central to Roman historiography.
