# Military Superiority and Technological Advantage

Ancient historians analyzed how military organization, technology, and leadership affected historical outcomes.

## Definition and Core Concept

Military historians in antiquity examined how military superiority in organization, technology, or tactics determined outcomes. Roman historians emphasized Rome's military superiority. Xenophon analyzed military organization and effectiveness. This analysis contributed to understanding of how material conditions and organization shaped historical outcomes.
