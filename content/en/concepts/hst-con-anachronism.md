# Anachronism in Historical Writing

Historians sometimes projected later concepts, values, or categories back onto earlier periods, creating distortions.

## Definition and Core Concept

Anachronism involves applying later concepts or values to earlier periods where they did not exist or had different meanings. Ancient historians sometimes made anachronistic claims (describing earlier republics as having democratic institutions identical to their own). Modern historians recognize anachronism as a fundamental problem requiring careful awareness of historical difference.
