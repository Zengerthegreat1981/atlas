# Apocalyptic and Eschatological Thinking

Some ancient historians employed apocalyptic or eschatological frameworks viewing history as moving toward end-times.

## Definition and Core Concept

Jewish and Christian historians developed eschatological perspectives viewing history as moving toward divine judgment or transformation. These perspectives influenced how they interpreted historical events and their significance. Apocalyptic thinking shaped interpretation of historical crises and conflicts.
