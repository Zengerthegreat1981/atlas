# Legendary and Mythological Elements in Historical Accounts

Ancient historians sometimes included legendary and mythological material while attempting to distinguish it from historical fact.

## Definition and Core Concept

Many ancient accounts contained legendary and mythological material—accounts of gods, heroes, and marvelous events mixed with historical facts. Historians like Livy dealt with early Roman history that was partly legendary. The challenge lay in distinguishing legend from fact. Some historians rejected mythology entirely; others attempted to separate legendary elements from historical kernels.
