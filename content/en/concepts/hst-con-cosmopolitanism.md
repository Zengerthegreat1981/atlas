# Cosmopolitanism in the Hellenistic World

Hellenistic historiography developed more cosmopolitan perspectives incorporating multiple cultures.

## Definition and Core Concept

Hellenistic historiography, written after Alexander's conquests, developed more cosmopolitan perspectives acknowledging cultural diversity and interconnection. Historians like Polybius and Strabo wrote for audiences across the Hellenistic world. This cosmopolitanism represented an advance in cultural perspective, though still filtered through Greek assumptions.
