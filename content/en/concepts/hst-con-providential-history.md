# Providence and Divine Will in Ancient History

Some ancient historians attributed historical outcomes to divine providence while others emphasized natural causation.

## Definition and Core Concept

Ancient historians disagreed about the role of divine will in historical causation. Some, like Livy, occasionally invoked divine punishment for moral transgressions. Others, like Thucydides, excluded supernatural explanation. The tension between providential and naturalistic causation ran through ancient historiography. Christian historians later emphasized providential history more consistently.
