# Plausibility and Rational Evaluation

Ancient historians evaluated accounts for plausibility—whether they made sense given other knowledge.

## Definition and Core Concept

Plausibility testing involved asking whether accounts made sense given what was known about human nature, military possibilities, or other factors. Implausible claims were viewed skeptically. However, plausibility testing could lead to false conclusions if modern historians' sense of what's plausible differed from ancient reality.
