# Virtue and Vice as Historical Causes

Ancient historians often explained historical outcomes through the virtue or vice of leaders and peoples.

## Definition and Core Concept

The attribution of historical outcomes to virtue (moral excellence) or vice (moral corruption) was a common pattern in ancient historiography. Livy explained Rome's success through virtue and its decline through moral corruption. Plutarch revealed character through historical narrative. This moral causation framework emphasized personal character as historical force.
