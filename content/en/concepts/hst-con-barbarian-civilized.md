# Barbarian and Civilized in Ancient Historiography

Ancient historians made distinctions between 'civilized' (Greek/Roman) and 'barbarian' peoples, categories reflecting cultural bias.

## Definition and Core Concept

The distinction between 'barbarian' and 'civilized' in ancient historiography often reflected a view that Greek and Roman culture were civilized while other peoples were barbarian. This distinction served ideological purposes justifying conquest and cultural superiority. Modern historians recognize these categories as reflecting the historians' perspectives rather than objective cultural differences.
