# Mortality

Mortality is not concept to understand but condition to live. Existential philosophy brings awareness of death from abstract background into conscious life. Awareness that we will die, knowing not when, is foundational to authenticity.

## Being-Toward-Death

Heidegger called authentic existence "being-toward-death": accepting death not as eventual event but constant horizon. We are always dying; each moment brings us closer. This is not morbid but clarifying—distinguishes what truly matters from mere distraction.

## Denial and Authenticity

Much inauthenticity structures denial of mortality. We speak as having unlimited time; postpone what matters; treat life as rehearsal. Mortality awareness shatters illusions and forces the question: What would I do differently accepting limited time?

Yalom's work suggests moving from intellectual to felt awareness catalyzes significant change. Not dramatic interventions but genuine reordering of priorities, increased authenticity, and clarified values.
