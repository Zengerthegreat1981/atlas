# Presentism and Teleological History

The tendency to interpret past events as leading inevitably to the present creates distortions in historical understanding.

## Definition and Core Concept

Presentism interprets the past from the perspective of the present, viewing history as a progression toward current conditions. Teleological history treats the past as a series of steps leading inevitably to present outcomes. Ancient historians were not entirely free from such thinking; Roman historians sometimes presented Rome's rise as inevitable; later historians viewing Rome's fall were sometimes presented as inevitable decline.
