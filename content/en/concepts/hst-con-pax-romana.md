# Pax Romana and Imperial Peace

Roman imperial historians sometimes presented the empire as establishing a lasting peace.

## Definition and Core Concept

The concept of Pax Romana presented the Roman Empire as having created stable, peaceful conditions. While the empire did reduce warfare within its territory, this narrative sometimes obscured violence, oppression, and conflicts. The Pax Romana was an interpretation emphasizing stability at the cost of freedom and independence.
