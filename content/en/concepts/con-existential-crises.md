# Existential Crises

An existential crisis is a period of acute confrontation with fundamental existential realities—mortality, freedom, isolation, meaninglessness—that can no longer be avoided. These are distinct from (though may accompany) clinical crises like suicidality.

## Characteristics

Existential crises involve:
- Sudden awareness that one's life is not authentically one's own
- Confrontation with mortality and the finiteness of time
- Loss of previously meaningful frameworks (relationships, work, beliefs)
- Acute awareness of radical freedom and responsibility
- Breakdown of defensive structures that previously maintained the illusion of security or meaning

## Therapeutic Work

The existential therapist views crisis as potential turning point. Rather than merely stabilizing the person and returning them to their previous state, the question becomes: What is this crisis revealing? What do I need to change in how I am living?

Crisis, properly engaged, can lead to reorganization at a higher level of authenticity and integration.
