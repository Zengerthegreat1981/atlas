# Eyewitness Participation in Historical Narrative

Ancient historians valued their own participation in events they narrated, claiming superior knowledge from direct experience.

## Definition and Core Concept

Eyewitness participation was a source of authority for ancient historians. Thucydides emphasized his participation in the Peloponnesian War. Xenophon claimed authority from leading the Ten Thousand. Participation gave historians direct knowledge unavailable to later historians. However, participation could also introduce bias and limited perspective.
