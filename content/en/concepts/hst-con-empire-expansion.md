# Empire-Building and Imperial Expansion

Ancient historians analyzed the processes and consequences of imperial expansion.

## Definition and Core Concept

Roman historians devoted considerable attention to explaining Rome's imperial expansion and analyzing its consequences. Polybius examined how Rome defeated rivals. Livy narrated Rome's territorial growth. Tacitus analyzed imperial rule. Imperial historians explained how empires developed, consolidated power, and administered diverse territories.
