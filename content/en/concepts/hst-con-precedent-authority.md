# Precedent and Authority in Roman Governance

Roman historians analyzed how precedent and established authority affected governance and political change.

## Definition and Core Concept

Roman political culture emphasized precedent and established authority (mos maiorum—the custom of the ancestors). Historians like Livy and Tacitus analyzed how precedent constrained political action and how innovations challenged traditional authority. Understanding precedent and authority was essential for understanding Roman politics.
