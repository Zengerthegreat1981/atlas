# Religious History and Theological Narrative

Ancient historians incorporated religious beliefs, practices, and theological ideas into historical narratives.

## Definition and Core Concept

Religion was important in ancient historiography. Historians described religious practices, divine will, and how religious beliefs motivated action. Later Christian historians developed more explicitly theological approaches to history, viewing history as divine providence. The relationship between religious belief and historical narrative remained complex.
