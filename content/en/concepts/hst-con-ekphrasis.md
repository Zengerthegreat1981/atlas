# Ekphrasis: Detailed Description in Historical Writing

Ancient historians used detailed, vivid description (ekphrasis) to bring events and places to life for readers.

## Definition and Core Concept

Ekphrasis, or detailed descriptive writing, was a rhetorical technique ancient historians employed to create vivid pictures of events, places, and artifacts. Detailed descriptions of battles, temples, or cities helped readers visualize historical events. However, the desire for vividness could lead to embellishment or distortion for literary effect.
