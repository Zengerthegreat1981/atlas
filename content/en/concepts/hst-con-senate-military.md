# Senate and Military Authority in Roman History

Ancient historians analyzed the relationship between senatorial authority and military power in Roman politics.

## Definition and Core Concept

The balance between senatorial authority and military power was crucial in Roman history. When military commanders controlled armies, they could challenge senatorial authority (Caesar crossing the Rubicon). Historians analyzed how changes in military and senatorial authority affected political development and stability.
