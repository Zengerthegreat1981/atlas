# Internal Consistency in Historical Accounts

Ancient historians evaluated accounts for internal consistency as a test of reliability.

## Definition and Core Concept

Accounts that were internally consistent and did not contradict other established facts were regarded as more reliable. Logical inconsistency within an account or contradiction with other evidence suggested error or falsehood. Internal consistency testing was an important method of source evaluation.
