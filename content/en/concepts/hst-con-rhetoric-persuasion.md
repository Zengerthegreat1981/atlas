# Rhetoric and Persuasion in Political History

Ancient historians recognized that rhetorical skill and persuasive ability shaped political outcomes.

## Definition and Core Concept

Ancient historians, trained in rhetoric, recognized rhetoric's power in political affairs. Speeches in historical narrative revealed how leaders persuaded others. The power of rhetoric—both true and false persuasion—affected historical outcomes. Understanding rhetoric became essential for understanding political history.
