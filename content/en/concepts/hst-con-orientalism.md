# Orientalism in Ancient Historiography

Ancient historians sometimes portrayed eastern peoples and cultures through stereotypes reflecting Greek and Roman prejudices.

## Definition and Core Concept

Orientalism in ancient historiography involved representing eastern peoples through stereotypes and prejudices. Persian 'luxury,' Egyptian 'mystery,' Scythian 'savagery' were repeated tropes that reflected Greek and Roman perspectives rather than accurate description. Modern historians recognize this as a pattern reflecting the historians' cultural biases.
