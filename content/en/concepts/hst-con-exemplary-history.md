# Exemplary History and Moral Instruction

Ancient historians often presented history as providing moral exempla—examples of virtue and vice to emulate or avoid.

## Definition and Core Concept

Exemplary history used historical figures and events as moral examples. This approach emphasized history's moral purpose. Historians like Plutarch and Valerius Maximus explicitly used history for moral instruction. The challenge lay in avoiding distortion of facts to fit moral purposes.
