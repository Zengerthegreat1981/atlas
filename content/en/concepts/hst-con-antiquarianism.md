# Antiquarianism and Archaeological Interest

Some ancient writers pursued antiquarian interest in past artifacts, inscriptions, and physical remains.

## Definition and Core Concept

Antiquarianism involved systematic interest in collecting and interpreting ancient artifacts, inscriptions, and physical remains. Pausanias's description of Greek temples and sculptures combined antiquarian and historical interests. This approach prefigured modern archaeology and material culture studies.
