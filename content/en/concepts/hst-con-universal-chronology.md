# Universal Chronology and Synchronization

Later ancient historians attempted to create universal chronologies synchronizing all peoples' chronologies.

## Definition and Core Concept

Creating a universal chronology that synchronized all peoples' chronological systems was an ambitious project attempted by ancient historians. Eusebius of Caesarea developed extensive chronological tables attempting this coordination. The difficulty lay in resolving conflicting chronologies and establishing absolute dates.
