# Isonomia and Political Equality in Ancient Government

Ancient historians analyzed different conceptions of political equality and the organization of government.

## Definition and Core Concept

Isonomia (equal distribution of political power) was a Greek ideal that historians analyzed. How different states organized political participation and decision-making was important to understanding their political systems. Historical change sometimes involved shifts in how power was distributed.
